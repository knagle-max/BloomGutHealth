# Nutrient source definitions — first v0.6 M1 slice

`shared/nutrient-definitions.ts` projects the existing21 `nutrientDefinitions` tuples into typed definitions: stable analyte/source ID, canonical amount unit, chemical form/activity basis where actually specified by the USDA field, source mapping and definition version. No second nutrient registry/store, new corpus, CNF redistribution, nutrient/portion calculation, source mutation or target/conversion function. CNF mappings stay null; absent iodine/niacin/B6 and other fields are not invented or zero-filled. Full M1 observation/form provenance remains OPEN.

The source definitions distinguish USDA1106 RAE from unconverted vitamin A,1177 total folate from DFE,1114 D2+D3 micrograms,1109 alpha-tocopherol from unverified activity/isomers, and1185 phylloquinone from all vitamin K/menaquinones. They describe source fields; an available name or amount does not prove a label formulation. Product/mixed/legacy sources conservatively show form/basis not recorded, including Vitamin A/Folate basis-unconfirmed display names. Original saved quantities/names remain untouched. No RAE/DFE/NE/IU or compound→elemental conversion or absorbed-dose estimate is added.

Three-layer UX: existing meal review nutrient table remains the surface with original known/unknown/coverage and explicit uncertain basis; Nutrition sources & science→Nutrient forms & units opens original field meanings and official USDA documentation within two interactions; missing/label/ambiguous sources explain unrecorded forms and offer the existing original-label/specific-source review rather than invented equivalents. A displayed zero can reflect rounding; this slice never labels it source-verified zero. Clinical deficiency, adequacy, usual intake, UL safety and supplement dose are not derived from these estimates. No added dashboard or pressure alert.
