`shared/genetics-catalogue.ts` is the versioned static navigation/type adapter over the existing 29 explanations and their reference placements. Existing explanatory content is reused, not copied into a second interpretation engine. Candidate IDs are stable, multi-domain membership refers to one candidate, and gene-only nodes never manufacture SNP IDs or measured calls. The current named supplement comes from the explicit Architect decision of 2026-11-04. It does not substitute for the unavailable complete Biology §5A inventory.

Actual counts: 38 variant/complex candidates (29 existing educational records, nine pending new candidates), plus 15 gene-only research topics. New independently reviewed candidates: zero. New personal action rules: zero. Minimum target is 150 and current shortfall is 112; maximum target 250. Bone/connective tissue and healthy-aging domains are explicit empty states pending sourcing. These counts are not clinical approval, assay verification, measured calls or successful personal interpretations.

The typed identity supports SNVs with explicit reference placement, uncurated insertion identity, deletion, copy number, repeat, phase-dependent haplotype, HLA typing, structural repeat and star-allele representation. New entries have no curated call mapping and cannot be inferred from ordinary raw SNP absence. No unprovided PGx gene/star allele was invented merely to populate a type.
