`shared/genetics-relevance.ts` supplies a strict versioned variant–phenotype claim contract and deterministic eligibility-before-ordering selector. A claim needs source consent, a single observed call, exact normalized study contrast/forward placement/build, verified compatible assay, exact approved claim version, population transportability and nonmissing evidence accounting. Clinical/PGx/research-only and APOE claims cannot become ordinary wellness highlights. Unknown effect size/CI stay null. Source calls and education are never rewritten.

Evidence credibility precedes exact recorded goal and active canonical laboratory-marker context, with stable gene/claim ties. No numeric health/risk score, rarity bonus, inference of deficiency, additive correlated effects or plan mutation. FADS related markers collapse unless independently reviewed distinct effects are explicitly referenced; duplicate variants show once. Five is a ceiling, not a quota. See All preserves eligible claims separately, with related-effect warning and relevance/evidence/gene sorts.

Production claim registry is deliberately empty: existing descriptions/bibliography/legacy `reviewed` flags are not independent claim approvals. Current manual/raw source contracts also lack verified assay evidence. No real personal highlight is enabled by this commit. The actual report adapter keeps literal assay notes unverified, uses current active source only, and supplies separately recorded goals/canonical active lab IDs for relevance only. Personal-qualified variants, matched educational comparisons, measured calls, catalogue candidates, reviewed claim records and eligible claims have separate counts.
