## 2. Key AI/ML amendments (map to BIO-VPA and existing canonical work; DO NOT create a second model registry, body ledger, provider stack or consent store)

### ML-VPA-2401 — Acquisition privacy and raw/derived distinction (VPA-01/02/04)
**Issue:** The handoff says perform on-device EXIF/GPS stripping and orientation normalization while preserving a 'raw source'. If a raw original with GPS/identifiers is persistently stored, 'metadata stripped' can mislead.
**Amendment:** Default to stripping metadata **before persistent upload/storage** and store the resulting unwarped, orientation-corrected, content-preserving image as the canonical private photographic source, together with a server-generated original-to-derived record. Do not persist an unredacted camera original or GPS EXIF by default. If a user specifically elects to retain an original including embedded metadata, require a distinct disclosure, protection and deletion contract; preferably avoid offering this at first. Previews/crops/segmentation remain derived records with expiry and owner checks. EXIF-free ≠ anonymous, and face masking does not prove anonymity.
**Test:** Inspect actual stored blob bytes and object metadata, upload retries, signed URLs, browser previews, exports, thumbnail cache and deletion; no location EXIF in default originals or derived assets. Camera/OS/browser local caches and backups documented truthfully.

### ML-VPA-2402 — Explainable image-comparability without artificial change (VPA-03/04)
**Issue:** Image registration can create apparent body-size differences; a smooth visual slider does not establish anatomic equivalence.
**Amendment:** Compare images only within compatible viewpoint/pose, physical framing and capture-quality strata. Track camera intrinsics if available, pose landmark quality, clothing/occlusion, orientation, zoom/distance consistency and segmentation uncertainty. Never stretch/warp the person or infer centimetres from pixel widths alone. Permit manual visual comparison even when automated numeric comparison abstains. Version and disclose reasons for uncomparable pairs.
**Test:** Same-person changed lens/distance, zoom, posture, lighting, clothing, camera tilt and duplicate images must not produce confident size-change assertions; graceful no-estimate. Distinguish image-space ratios from physical measures.

### ML-VPA-2403 — Per-site, longitudinal validation beyond cross-sectional MAE (VPA-05/06/08)
**Issue:** Low single-timepoint circumference MAE or correlation can coexist with large false-progress errors between visits. Regulatory/public accuracy cannot borrow published model claims.
**Amendment:** For each waist/hip/arm/thigh and each capture protocol, pre-register ground-truth measurement site, clinical/reference procedure, hardware, target population, error tolerance, required smallest detectable change and abstention coverage. Subject-level and preferably site/device/time-disjoint holdouts. Evaluate **delta error** (predicted change minus reference change), false change alarms, longitudinal repeatability, Bland–Altman limits, absolute and relative bias, calibration/interval coverage if probabilistic, subgroup reliability, manual-tape baseline, and rejection of out-of-domain captures. Freeze thresholds before examining holdout. A model may be approved for a single site/protocol only, not general anthropometry.
**Test:** Paired repeat captures with zero true tape change and controlled real changes, baseline tape/no-photo, independently blinded references; model fails or abstains when measurement error exceeds supported real change. No absolute fat %, localized muscle or health inference from photos.

### ML-VPA-2404 — Feedback labels and account-private calibration (VPA-05/07)
**Issue:** Corrections are nonrandom, prediction-anchored and often guesses. Training on them as unbiased measurements can degrade a model.
**Amendment:** Typed labels: `model_suggestion`, `accepted_without_measurement`, `user_visual_adjustment`, `user_reported_tape_with_site_protocol`, `independently_observed_reference`. Capture model version, displayed suggestion, edit order/timestamps and correction purpose. No edit or acceptance becomes gold truth. Calibrate privately only from eligible actual comparable tape entries (with quality/uncertainty handling); benchmark against **no-calibration** and a simple personal offset, not only a complex adaptive model. User can remove personal learned parameters and derived estimates without deleting source photos, with independent account/consent invalidation.
**Test:** Mass accepts/rounded edits do not falsely boost apparent accuracy; erroneous tape entry correction reverses downstream calibration; cold start and rare-history accounts default to appropriate manual mode.

### ML-VPA-2405 — Local inference and operational cost/compatibility (VPA-04/06)
**Amendment:** First benchmark small, suitably licensed on-device pose/segmentation tools against a manual crop/guide baseline on actual supported iPhone Safari, Android and desktop devices. Measure model asset download, cold/warm latency, memory, energy, failure on WebGPU/WASM/camera denial and accessibility. No mandatory third-party provider. Any self-hosted optional inference stays behind the existing private server, capability/cost/permission and output-review controls, with bounded payloads and no cloud imagery by default. Python research is allowed without requiring public model inference.
**Test:** With local AI unavailable, photo journal/manual tape/comparison remain usable; low-end devices do not crash; network traces show no body image sent to research endpoints or AI providers without purpose-specific consent.

### ML-VPA-2406 — Separate photo storage, analysis, and shared-training permissions (VPA-01/07)
**Amendment:** Treat private image storage, on-device landmark computation, optional server inference, retained per-account calibration and future contribution to shared training as separate permission purposes. Future raw photo contribution is a separate higher-risk decision from derived features. Revalidate permissions before queued compute, commit, view and export; stale jobs never repopulate revoked photos/estimates. A global trained-model deletion/unlearning promise must accurately describe technical limits and immutable release histories. Shared learning remains OFF pending explicit owner + Security + Legal authorization.
**Test:** revoke during upload and analysis, account switch, delayed thumbnail generation, expired signed URL, malformed cache key, foreign user access, training toggle OFF; no cross-account image/feature exposure and no old derived result reappears.

### ML-VPA-2407 — No auto biological inference or advice from appearance (VPA-03/06/08)
**Amendment:** Keep separate display categories: original photograph; acquisition QA; pixel/silhouette observation; user tape measurement; experimental numerical estimate; body-composition or causal interpretation. Only reviewed canonical *observations* may inform Progress on their own terms. Do not infer energy balance, fat change, regional muscle growth, obesity/disease risk, exercise recovery or personal dietary response from image differences. Generic evidence-approved lifestyle advice can proceed independently of image analysis, not because a hypothetical body measurement was treated as ground truth.
**Test:** image difference with unchanged tape and unchanged training, conflicting scale measures, editing a photo date, and missing data never generate personalized calorie change, treatment claim, fat % or muscle gain claim.

### ML-VPA-2408 — Implementation-status and staged value gate (VPA-01–08)
**Amendment:** Delivery ladder: (1) versioned private photos/manual body measurements with secure export/delete; (2) standardized capture and useful nonwarped comparison; (3) local QA/pose and optional qualitative image-space observations; (4) shadow research site-specific estimates and benchmark receipts; (5) qualified narrow model activation; (6) separately authorized collective learning. Track code/CI/live verified/scientifically validated/security accepted/release authorized as independent statuses. Do not relabel Stage A as all Visual AI completed, but don't hold Stage A for Stage C accuracy. Preserve existing NEXT_ACTIONS task priority and don't recreate a blanket approval loop.
**Test:** Real account A/B, fresh mobile capture, save/reopen/export/delete, manual chart integration and disabled-model fallback; separately benchmark true incremental user benefit (repeatable capture, ease of tracking, non-misleading comprehension) against photos + tape baseline.
