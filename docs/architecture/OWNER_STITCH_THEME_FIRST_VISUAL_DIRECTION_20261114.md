## Single visual system; avoid splitting into two themes
- **Palette:** warm ivory `#faf9f7` with gradated ivory/paper layers (`#f4f3f1`, `#efeeec`, white); deep forest `#012d1d`, botanical forest `#1b4332`, leaf/sage `#3f6653`, soft sage `#d1e4de`, restrained amber/gold `#dd9832` / `#ffddb6`, neutral body `#1a1c1b`. Maintain semantic color tokens and dark-mode counterparts; don't treat pale gray overlays as low-contrast body text.
- **Typography:** expressive **Newsreader** for greetings, editorials, hero and section titles; crisp **Plus Jakarta Sans** for actions/content and numerics; optional restrained monospace/technical micro-label only in *genuine* deeper Biology/Explore/Progress scientific detail, NOT across every screen or headline. Distinctive hierarchy and legibility; don't make all numbers giant dashboard KPIs.
- **Material:** tactile shallow inset wells, printed-paper surfaces, fine hairlines/rules, debossed segmented controls, subtly elevated ivory ceramic/satin cards and botanical/dark-forest anchor surfaces. Use shadows selectively; not uniform padded card stacks, double outlines or every section boxed.
- **Graphic:** bespoke thin editorial schematics, elegant small capillary charts, flowing connecting pathway lines and controlled gold scientific highlights; actual food photography with ceramic/wood daylight tactile lighting. No stock clinic/scifi neon, anonymous avatars, generic icon spray or crowded badges.
- **Rhythm:** generous breathing room for editorial overview, purposeful compactness in data-dense ledger. Avoid tiny 9–11px body text and visually exhausting full-screen table sequences, especially in 320px designs. Vary open content, dark botanical feature, paper ledgers and inset control rows.
- **Navigation:** consistent 5-route visual shell (Today/Food/Biology/Explore/Progress) while preserving existing profile, Athlete and other functional routes and desktop presentation. Header/logo consistent, non-obtrusive; no decorative fake 'Live Bio-Link' indicator unless actual meaningful status; Codex handles actual status semantics. Legible active destination.
- **Accessibility:** allow zoom and text scaling (prototype `user-scalable=no` is not acceptable), light/dark, reduced motion, touch, keyboard and safe-area behavior. Code uses real design tokens/assets not runtime Tailwind CDN or prototype remote-image hotlinks.
