## Binding implementation — LEG-USE-1301–1306
1. **One registry, owned jointly by Architect/Legal:** reuse 52-feature IDs, no second release flag system. Every presently accessible feature and future recommendation/AI output/API and corresponding copy must link to a feature ID, purpose, exact inputs and influence on ranking, example outputs, actual user decisions and consequences, source/assay caveats, marketing and UI claim text, evidence IDs/status, intended audience/territory, separate Health Canada/FDA rationale, reviewer and release approval. New functionality gets a new stable feature ID.
2. **Risk is output-specific:** log ordinary, missing and hazardous examples. Explicitly distinguish educational display of sourced LDL/ALT or microbiome taxon from disease-directed recommendation/diagnosis/urgency/therapy. Laboratory/genomic/microbiome **triggering personalized recommendations** must be reviewed on concrete example codepaths, not waved through as wellness. Medical output uncertainty remains UNDETERMINED; don't invent physician approvals.
3. **Claims/evidence parity:** indexed public/UI/landing/app-store claims and generated examples must never exceed supported evidence or reviewed scope. LLM outputs must not freely turn exploratory pathways or associations into medical instructions. Avoid one size fits all 'health optimization' claim as substitute for actual text.
4. **Canada and U.S. recorded independently:** assign potentially device-related claims to focused qualified counsel assessment before affected-jurisdiction public release; record outcome, rationale, date, links and territorial enforcement. U.S. CDS criteria aimed at non-device professional decision support cannot be automatically asserted for patient-directed decisions.
5. **Activation:** new claims/functions with plausible medical purpose remain unavailable publicly (including direct endpoints/API/AI/export/scheduled jobs) until jurisdiction/clinical/privacy/security and evidence checks, explicit authorization and tests are recorded. This does not order blanket disabling of existing benign live features merely because the initial legal draft has false review flags. Escalate presently exposed high-consequence output promptly for actual review and containment.
6. **Change triggers:** new claims, population, medical data inputs, intervention rankings, model/vendor, jurisdiction, marketing copy or evidence require focused review of affected feature, with versioned snapshots, not reopening the whole backlog.

## Risk-tiered initial queue (not legal classifications)
- **First examine** lab- or microbiome/genotype-triggered individualized advice (LDL/ALT disease contexts, genetics-derived treatment/prescription), clinical thresholds, urgent alerts, supplementation dose suggestions, symptom triage and future digital-twin/flux outputs. Store real normal/borderline/adverse examples and what action is induced.
- **Then** general nutrition and exercise recommendations, meal plans, training load/recovery, allergy avoidance and generative AI summaries, with safety/privacy/licensing checks.
- **Lower review burden for likely informational utilities:** user-entered food/workout logging and source-preserving lab/microbiome display and trends, subject to exact wording and non-diagnostic behavior. Not a blanket legal clearance.
