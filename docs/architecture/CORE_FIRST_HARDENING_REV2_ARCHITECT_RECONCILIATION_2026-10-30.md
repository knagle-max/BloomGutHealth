## Product intention and claim governance
**Controlling Owner scope:** evidence-based **personal wellness**; people can explore research and receive relevant non-diagnostic food, activity and lifestyle suggestions informed by their real data. Bloom does **not** intend to diagnose, treat, monitor or prescribe for diseases; physicians handle clinical directions. A rule using labs/genetics/microbiome is **not categorically forbidden**, but a specific output with disease/therapeutic medical purpose needs appropriate substantiation and jurisdiction-specific intended-use review. Preserve explanatory genetic/microbe research separately from unvalidated personalized ranking; quarantine *specific* unsupported rules rather than disable all useful personalization. Use existing 52-feature intended-use register; no duplicative regulatory framework.
