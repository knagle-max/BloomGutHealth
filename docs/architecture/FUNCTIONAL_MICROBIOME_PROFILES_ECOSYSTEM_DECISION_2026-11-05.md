## Architectural ruling — one existing research/profile graph, not a new diagnostic engine
Map BIO-FUNC-01–07 into **existing** BIO-TAXON-2901–2905 and MICRO-06, Science Explorer, organism detail and taxonomy/evidence claims; map UT-FUNC-4601–4612 to existing UT-TAXON-2801–2808, UT-LIVE-3801–3810, UT-VIOME-4301–4312, UT-MICROFOOD-4501–4512 and current source/provenance/consent. **No duplicated organism-profile service, microbial knowledge graph, personal nutrition recommendation engine or synthetic gut-health score.** Treat this as useful evidence-backed educational navigation for preserved source observations, with no inference of a person's ecosystem activity, diet, metabolites or medical status.

**Five non-interchangeable evidence states** on each row/claim: (1) **reported by your assay** (qualitative identity only unless original measured quantitative fields exist); (2) **capability in published studies** (which taxon, strain, model, population and experimental conditions); (3) **pathway/gene actually measured for this source** only if validated provider assay payload supplies it; (4) **expression/activity actually measured in this source** only if supplied; (5) **metabolite or functional concentration actually measured in this source** only if supplied, with units/method. The existence of state (1) cannot imply (3)–(5). `not_reported` is not zero, missing is not absent, and a source provider's `Attention/Improve/Maintain` rating is **not** a measured butyrate concentration or activity. Do not call a qualitative RNA-listed microbe a validated strain isolate, or silently relabel RNA as DNA shotgun.

## Scope and priorities
**v0.5 P0 scientific correctness for anything already displayed:** Preserve original full taxon/strain-like name, group, assay resolution, qualitative observed status and date; do not infer relative abundance/rank/diversity, SCFA flux, community balance, beneficial-to-pathogenic ratio, toxin gene/BFT, actual microbial co-culture interaction, measured metabolite or diagnosis from a presence-only report. No automatic diet prescription, infection alarm, harmless assurance or high-fibre/plant-consumption conclusion from taxa. Keep independently measured quantitative reports accurately labelled as quantitative. In unreviewed research areas, show verified source taxon identity and neutral education/unknowns **without blocking the whole profile**, but withhold specific unsupported variant/strain–function/clinical effect claims. Correct/update/retract claims without modifying source observations; remove stale summaries when source/review status changes. These are truthfulness and privacy gates, **not a blanket per-taxon clinical signoff to display its measured/reported identity**.

**v0.5 P1 useful experience, bounded:** On `My Biology → Gut reports → Organisms` and existing organism profiles, allow browseable **functional groups** (e.g. SCFA-related pathways, polysaccharide processing, bile-acid transformations, mucin ecology, mixed/unknown and viral/fungal context). Group memberships are overlapping and evidence-specific; never universal good/bad species rankings. **At most a small initial selection** of interesting reviewed, source-observed items where available (UT example 3–5 is an optional UX hypothesis, not a quota; 0 is allowed if no qualified claims, while all original reported taxa remain visible). “Explore all functional groups” and search of every reported organism always available. Selected profile: `What your report shows` → `Roles studied in research` → `Compounds and pathways` → `Possible food/substrate relationships` → `Evidence and limitations` → original report. Maintain navigation/back context and existing original source lists; no re-layout of whole My Biology.

**Cross-feeding / ecosystem graph (P1):** Model candidate edges separately: Producer → molecule (e.g., studied lactate/acetate production), molecule → consumer (e.g., separate laboratory lactate utilization). Both edges require exact reviewed literature grounding and taxon/strain/population/model conditions. Co-occurrence in the user's report **does not establish actual cross-feeding** or personal network flux. Mark composite relationship `potential ecological interaction based on studies`. Only direct coculture or suitably designed interaction study may substantiate studied direct interaction; never infer that a person's stool reflects that experiment. Provide accessible text list of edges with independently linked source references, not visually-only arrows.

**Food links (P1):** A microbe's ability to metabolize a culture substrate is not evidence that eating a food increases it or improves clinical outcomes. Keep culture mechanism, observational study, human intervention, provider food list and a validated personal nutrition recommendation **separate**. Optional `Use my microbiome food recommendations` account-level Off/On policy affects only ranking through one existing resolver; no microbiome-food label automatically validates a mechanism. Allergies/medical restrictions and user exclusions take precedence. Food suggestions without observed causal support use neutral research language, not a treatment claim.

**v0.6 / separately gated:** Quantitative community function, individualized interaction prediction, SCFA flux/metabolite inference, advanced ecosystem simulations and clinical dietary intervention rules require appropriate validated assay inputs, model/outcome evidence and separate scientific/legal approval; **not implemented by adding a diagram**.

## Explicit examples requiring careful science framing
- `Faecalibacterium prausnitzii`: studied butyrate-producing isolates, not personally abundant nor measured personal butyrate output.
- `Dysosmobacter welbionis`: a studied strain-level myo-inositol/butyrate pathway and separate human observational and animal evidence, **not** a personal tested pathway or treatment.
- `Akkermansia muciniphila`: documented mucin ecology does not establish personal barrier repair, mucus restoration or leanness.
- `Bacteroides` taxa: studied carbohydrate-use diversity; reported listing does not establish high-fibre intake or healthy ratios.
- `[Clostridium] scindens`: studied bile-acid transformations, not universally beneficial.
- `B. fragilis` reference-like 638R/YCH46 names: no proof of bft/ETBF or BFT toxin or isolate identity from provider label alone.
- `Bifidobacterium` and `Anaerostipes`: potential two-edge cross-feeding literature, not personal measured exchange.
- `Streptococcus pyogenes`, `C. perfringens`, oral-associated and fungal/viral labels: neither diagnose disease nor assure harmlessness. Plant viruses do not prove vegetable volume, food timing, diet quality or clinical infection. The handoffs' specific literature citations must be checked at claim-level before release.
