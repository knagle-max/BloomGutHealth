# Bloom — Day-One Future Research Participation Preference
**Date:** 2026-11-14 | **Status:** Controlling owner amendment, APPROVED for existing Account/Privacy settings implementation (not research processing)
**Priority:** P1 small scoped UX/backend delta while normal Food/Today/Response and security development continues
**Supersedes:** Earlier `V06_HANDOFF.md` §20, `RESEARCH_READY_MULTIJURISDICTION_DATA_FOUNDATION_20261114.md` and `NEXT_ACTIONS.md` language that prohibited *any* research-related preference control at initial launch. **Only the early preference control is newly authorized**; prohibition on activating study recruitment, secondary processing, cross-account pooling, third-party access and shared model training remains fully in force.

## Product intent and exact semantics
Owner wants the **toggle introduced early** so users can express a future research inclination and maintain useful longitudinal records today rather than later discovering the app discarded necessary *ordinary* records. This must be implemented truthfully: a one-bit unspecified toggle cannot constitute informed consent for unknown future research recipients/purposes, particularly sensitive health/genomic data or cross-border transfers.

**Approved default-Off control (existing Profile/Account → Privacy or Preferences; no new navigation):**
> **Hear about future research opportunities** [Off / On]
> “If Bloom offers optional health research in the future, I’m open to being contacted about it. Turning this on does not share my health information or enroll me in a study. You can change this at any time.”
If there is no approved research-contact communications channel or contact-related processing purpose in the deployed region, display a nonfunctional informative state or defer this control until the necessary contact policy exists; do **not** depict it as already enrolled or “data sharing on.” Minimal contact preference is independently safer than claiming broader research consent.

**Alternative future opt-in categories are DESIGN-ONLY** until specific approved research programs exist: Bloom internal population-model improvement; noncommercial university research; sponsored commercial/pharma research; historical records and their dates/types; international transfer/recipients; recontact; sensitive genomic/biome use. Do not present phantom permissions that imply a purpose is already valid. An early preference never supersedes later separate explicit study/category/recipient/time-range authorization. Revoking the preference prevents new nonessential research contact and doesn't change ordinary product/data permissions.

**No loss of data does not mean indefinite retention.** Bloom should continue preserving user-appropriate, consented original personal health records, source lineage, changes, revisions and measurements needed for **existing product functionality**, following its lawful privacy notice, retention/export/deletion policy and user control. Do not retain extra raw DNA, PDFs, extremely granular location/sensor history or records beyond the existing purposes simply in hope of later research. Later, a user could explicitly authorize a bounded subset of lawfully retained *historical* records (specific types/dates) for a real independently governed study, if legal basis and collection notice permit. No retroactive authorization or automatic backfill.

## State and permission contract — reuse existing account settings/consent
- Preference state `futureResearchContactPreference: 'off'|'on'` (default off), with receipt fields `noticeVersion, changedAt, jurisdictionPolicyVersion`, active account ID, authenticated updater and audit/scope; reflect actual persisted state on reload, new device, export and deletion when appropriate. Prefer existing settings/consent store; do not create a second consent engine. No fake grant and no “enrolled,” “contributing data” or “research data enabled” flags.
- Separate **researchUsePermission = false** and **researchContactPermission** as permitted; an “On” contact preference *must not* make API/DB calls authorized to access research data. Explicit purpose-specific permission AND live independently approved study AND permitted territory/consent must eventually be required for all future secondary uses.
- UI must clearly distinguish choice of future contact from sharing: no ambiguous button named “Contribute my data now,” no auto-ticked setting, no dark pattern, no preselection in onboarding. Simple App settings placement, not Today / Food / Biology or an experimental-results switch.
- Ordinary usage works unchanged when preference is Off. Profile remains private; public, super-user and coach roles cannot read another account's choice. No third-party tools, customer support analytics or external communications program piggyback without a reviewed purpose.
- Canada and US launch policies evaluated separately. EU/EEA future requires stricter review of lawful bases, special-category rules, marketing/recontact and transfers. Eligibility may differ by user age, location and available notices; do not infer jurisdiction purely from IP.
