# Bloom — Privacy-Preserving Personal & Population Learning: Architect Decision
**Date:** 2026-11-14 | **Status:** APPROVED architecture compatibility and P1 personal response-learning refinements; shared population training/aggregates DEFERRED pending explicit separate owner privacy/product decision, Security/Legal review and appropriate consent.
**Owner question:** Can Bloom learn from accumulated user diet, exercise, microbiome and laboratory outcomes without weakening strict account isolation?
**Controlling pre-existing rule:** `docs/architecture/V06_HANDOFF.md` §20 explicitly prohibits a research network, study recruitment, researcher access, broad cohort exports, uncontrolled model-training reuse or assuming ordinary account consent confers research permission. This decision does **not** override that. It specifies safe user-level improvements and a future architectural extension only.

## Architectural answer
Yes, in two distinct data-protection domains:
- **Track A — within-account adaptive longitudinal learning: APPROVED now, subject to current purpose/consent.** Each user's canonical private observations remain owned, encrypted, source-versioned and authorized solely for that account. Existing V06-13, LEARN-01–06, RNET-DATA-01–05, Response Intelligence and action/outcome receipt contracts support private changes over time without cross-account pooling. A computed response profile and its models remain scoped to that user and are never globally exposed. Unknown intake, no logged exercise, diet plans and skipped supplement reporting must not become confirmed events.
- **Track B — collective evidence/model improvement: NOT AUTHORIZED FOR LIVE PERSONAL DATA YET.** Existing user sharing/analytics settings and "Show development features" do not imply consent to pool health/genetics/microbiome or train shared models. Later authorization may enable purpose-specific opt-in contribution of minimized scientifically validated features, ideally secure aggregation with privacy protections, only after legal/security/privacy threat modelling and real protections. No deployment of an account-spanning dataset/ML training job by this handoff.

## Track A implementation using only existing systems
1. Reuse existing `shared/lab-history.ts`, canonical Food entries, activity, regimen and experiment events, Progress/Response and action decision receipts; derive a stable owned **exposure–outcome observation window** with event effective/occurredAt, recordedAt, initial+followup sample source/date/method/unit; baseline, possible co-exposures, adherence coverage, revision and uncertainty. Do not assume a fixed global window for all markers.
2. A claim like “changing diet improved my LDL” is **not** established by uncontrolled before/after observations. Output `observed association / insufficient observations / possible contributor with published population support` with transparent confounders, not individualized causal contribution or percentages.
3. Each user can correct, exclude, explain, override or say whether a recommendation was practical, chosen, actually performed, continued, stopped or accompanied by a symptom; store exact event provenance, without treating a thumbs-up as verified biological benefit.
4. Maintain per-user preference/feasibility model updates and carefully qualified descriptive response patterns locally in their account scope; preserve prior versions, minimum comparability and deterministic safety veto. The approved evidence/action registry stays independent: personal observed patterns cannot silently approve a new scientific claim.
5. Give users *useful* observational feedback: paired lab changes where assay comparability supports it, action timeline and documented missing context, prospective low-risk experiments/measurements when appropriate, one valuable context question. No request for unnecessary genetic/omics tests.
6. Correction/deletion/source revocation, account switch and consent change invalidate all dependent cached per-user features, model state, action receipts and shadow analytics. Cross-account authorization is enforced at service/query/job/cache boundary, not merely UI.

## Track B future option (design only; no new live warehouse or broad analysis)
At a later explicit owner decision, choose **specific purpose** (e.g., improving recommendations vs producing generalizable research), opt-in flow, jurisdiction and permitted health data categories. Security/Legal review handling of sensitive health/genetics/microbiome and third parties. Separate account-private source tables from strictly governed, minimized contribution pipeline:
`consented eligible owned observations → purpose-limited secure feature construction with quality gate → contribution policy & privacy risk assessment → cohort/group summary or trained statistical model → held-out independent assessment + governance → approved model/version released into normal evidence/action engine`.
- Access to cross-account analysis through a dedicated audited narrowly authorized service, not regular super-user account privileges; no row-level browsing or exports. Physical isolation, token/identity separation, encryption, output checks, red-team extraction/reidentification tests and retained audit receipts.
- Prefer genuinely minimal cohort summaries or secured central computation for early small datasets; differential privacy may improve protection for released aggregates/training but requires epsilon/privacy budget, composition controls, membership-inference risk testing and source sample adequacy. **Federated learning is optional, not a guarantee of privacy**; gradient/model updates can reveal data without additional techniques.
- Genomic variants and rare conditions amplify re-identification risk; suppress rare strata, extreme/outlier attributes and highly granular cross-tabs. Distinguish anonymization from pseudonymization; neither is magical and legal handling depends on actual processing.
- Opt-in must be genuine, revocable for future processing, unbundled from core service, and not retroactively inferred; truthful communication on contributions already incorporated in earlier non-reversible published aggregates/model versions. Preserve proofs of source consent, purpose, deletion and downstream model invalidation/retraining policy.
- New shared models are **hypotheses until independently benchmarked**. Evaluate selection bias (self-selected health-focused users), missingness, regression to mean, clinical confounders, different labs/device and microbiome methods, different diets, medication exposure, baseline covariates, subgroup transportability and leakage across repeated observations from same person. Participant-level train/validation/test split; time-corrected prediction; compare against guidelines-only baseline and confounder-aware simple regression; prospective or randomized studies before individual causal effect claims.
- No automatic promotion of a shared learned association into a personal medical or nutrition treatment rule. Reuse AI-led scientific governance tiers: Tier 1/2 require reviewed population evidence and scope-specific policy; Tier 3 speculative models may be eligible for restricted research only after rights/consent; Tier 4 clinical gates remain mandatory.

## Test / completion plan
**Now (Codex existing work):**
1. Two-user isolation at SQL and API plus background derived state, exports and UI, different source/consent histories, cache key; include role/token forgery.
2. Same-account diet/exercise/weight changes and pre/post lab dates with incompatible units/methods; demonstrate descriptive and qualified correlation only; no invented outcomes or causes.
3. User reports action chosen but not performed; null/unknown ≠ zero, new meal not consumed automatically, no adherence assumption from planned regimen or notification.
4. Retrospective event after draw cannot be treated as occurring after draw; corrections invalidate only affected derived findings; revocation/deletion removes active personal profile and caches.
5. Personal preferences/feasibility changes improve eligible recommendation choices while deterministic hard safety and existing action evidence remain unchanged.
6. Produce real user-specific improvement/coverage receipts vs baseline and reported UX practicality, not fabricated uplift.
**Future only, after authorization:** privacy threat model, purpose consent and deletion, k/size and rare-combination suppression, aggregated budget protections where DP used, membership/model inversion attacks, sample bias, participant-held-out cohort metrics, external validation, model regression, reviewer/rights findings, recovery after revocation. Fail closed.
