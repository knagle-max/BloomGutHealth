## Binding owner-confirmed intended use
Bloom is intended to help people **optimize diet, training and recovery** by considering goals, evidence and reliable personal inputs including bloodwork, genetics, microbiome, food/activity/sleep and observed responses. Bloom is **not intended to diagnose, detect, prevent, monitor, manage or treat disease or suspected disease**. An ordinary food or workout suggestion is **not automatically forbidden** because reliable lab/microbiome context helped select it; conversely disclaimer/wellness labels do not legalize a medical decision. Assess actual functions, exact claims, decision consequence, and jurisdiction; qualified counsel review before external launch.

## Medical concerns and evidence-to-action
- Safety is a **boundary**, not Bloom's purpose. If a reported result appears concerning, present non-diagnostic guidance to seek professional medical interpretation; urgent/triage categories only under separately reviewed clinical rules. Never imply an abnormal value establishes a diagnosis or that improvement establishes cure.
- Personalization needs population-applicable **human intervention evidence** for actionable ordinary changes, with explicit eligibility, assay reliability, uncertainty and no causal/predicted individual therapeutic claim from mechanistic associations alone.
- Personal longitudinal feedback can inform cautious low-risk adjustments to everyday nutrition/training, without declaring the changes caused laboratory outcomes.

## Suggested concrete negative/positive scenario matrix, not clinical determinations
- LDL: may discuss evidence on food patterns and ordinary substitutions; cannot diagnose cardiovascular disease, prescribe drugs/supplement doses or claim treatment-specific LDL risk reduction.
- ALT: track actual measurement and discuss general fitness/nutrition goal; cannot diagnose fatty liver or devise an ALT-triggered liver disease treatment protocol.
- HbA1c: educational and general food/activity options; no diabetes diagnosis/clinical treatment algorithm.
- 16S/shotgun: show reported taxa and potential mechanisms as limited; may suggest human-evidence-supported fiber foods, no fabricated flux, taxon normalization prescriptions or guaranteed personal effects.
- Training/sleep: reviewed general recovery adjustment without medical exercise clearance or injury diagnosis.
- No omics: valid baseline plan, no invented personal microbial status.
- Reported concerning results: professional referral message as reviewed, no emergency risk classification from unapproved model.
