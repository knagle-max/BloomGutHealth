## Controlling owner amendment — build research-ready capture from day one, without secondary use (2026-11-14)

**Binding architectural correction:** A future population research program must not require Bloom to reconstruct lost diet/activity/supplement/medication/laboratory/microbiome histories. **Implement durable, versioned, first-party longitudinal data capture as part of the core product from its first working release**, consistent with each user's expectations, purpose-specific privacy policy, consent and reasonable retention. The *same existing canonical private source records* should support today's personal feedback and—**only if separately authorized in the future**—a versioned, selective research contribution. This does not authorize research pooling, cross-account analysis, a hidden research corpus, or retroactive consent.

**P0-now capture contract (extend existing schemas, not duplicate):**
- Record source-original observations and corrections with stable opaque IDs: account owner, observation kind, exact source and released edition, capturedAt/occurredAt/recordedAt, specimen/assay/method/units/meal amount and preparation/activity intensity where supplied, time zone, source revision, missingness/quality, original versus normalized form, actual/planned/recalled/unknown state and consent/purpose version. Store no speculative detail or raw sensitive report bytes beyond justified operational need.
- Maintain optional, minimal user-approved life-change events using existing Food/Activity/Medications/Supplements/Progress/Response records; preserve start/stop/estimated dates, retrospective recall uncertainty, multiple simultaneous changes, protected outcomes, follow-up measurements and observed nonresponse. **Never** infer that a proposed action occurred.
- Make ingestion/import idempotent, correction-aware, audit-trailed, account-bound and replayable; typed canonical identifier dictionaries and future standards mappings (LOINC/UCUM/FHIR/OMOP, CNF/FDC, genotype build/strand, microbiome taxonomy/assay) allow later research extraction without falsely claiming current standards conformance.
- Version feature derivations, lab comparability, evidence/rule versions, data normalizers and any per-account model outputs. Preserve raw **legitimate** source measurements and event revisions under transparent product retention, while rebuilding derived views on edits/withdrawals. Explicit null/unknown and coverage are required to avoid fabricating dataset completeness.
- Record **historical permission truth**, not fictitious research grants: what service data-use notice/consent actually covered, when, and what categories were used. Do not fabricate retroactive participant consent, pre-check academic/commercial use, alter source-purpose automatically, or treat retained historic personal records as already shareable.
- **Future consent semantics:** A person could later opt into a specific research purpose, choose whether eligible *existing* historical records may be used and the permitted date range/data types, or contribute only prospectively. Before any historical sharing, reassess the original collection notice, applicable Canada/US/(future EU) law, lawful basis, permissions, sensitive genetic restrictions, ethics and purpose compatibility. Some older data may be ineligible regardless of later opt-in. Allow no contribution as a full product experience.
- Security: isolation and policy enforcement at SQL, jobs, caches, exports and AI/model boundaries; private first-party records not globally browsable by super-users or automatically available to research workers. No production cohort extraction or population training now.
- Retention/deletion: no indefinite speculative retention solely to create a future asset; respect transparent product retention, minimization and user deletion. If deletion means older data cannot be recovered for later research, **that is required privacy behavior, not a defect**. Do not secretly preserve deleted or otherwise unauthorized sources in a research backup.

**Day-one acceptance and proof:** Codex demonstrates two account-isolated imported longitudinal timelines; a user-initiated intervention date, optional corrected late recall, repeated lab source/method and microbial categorical report; unchanged old entries after CNF/provider version updates; null rather than zeros for unmeasured; lineage/correction replay; actual deletion and consent revocation; normal app workflows work with no research enrollment. A synthetic future-purpose eligibility/export test may validate technical readiness while its production permission remains hard-denied. Report source coverage and provenance counts, retention policy, exact test SHA and any capabilities not implemented.

**Explicit sequencing:** Integrate capture/provenance/replay into current Food #12, Response #15 and Adaptive Health Guidance now, not as a later research-only migration. Future cohort/research infrastructure, distinct donation/commercial consents and external partnerships remain a separately authorized phase. No extra owner checkpoint is needed for present safe product data engineering.

## Owner mission clarification — research in service of long, healthy lives (2026-11-14)

**Controlling product intent:** Bloom's ultimate purpose is to help people live longer, healthier lives by supporting well-informed decisions with scientifically trustworthy evidence. The purpose of any eventual academic/pharmaceutical partnership is **to advance useful knowledge and feed appropriately reviewed discoveries back into Bloom for the benefit of people**, not to monetize identifiable or individual users' health records. Commercial partnerships may fund legitimate scientific analyses, secure infrastructure, validation and product improvement, but revenue or sponsor demand must not override participant trust, privacy, independent evidence standards or scientific integrity.

**Research-to-Bloom translation:** permissioned, purpose-specific longitudinal observations → governed, disclosure-reviewed cohort analysis → replication, confounding/sensitivity tests, contrary/null/harm findings and externally valid scientific assessment → candidate evidence claims in the **existing** scientific-governance lifecycle → tier-appropriate independent checks, qualification/abstention and clinical review as applicable → versioned eligible evidence and explanations → safer/more useful Bloom guidance → evaluate actual user comprehension and relevant outcomes. No direct path from commercial result, cohort correlation or partner-funded hypothesis to an approved personalized recommendation. Publish negative/null findings where permitted; preserve research independence, disclosure of sponsorship and study limitations.

**Partnership principles:** Prefer controlled analysis / approved aggregate answers / secure compute-to-data over selling or exporting raw individual records. Support qualified academic/nonprofit research alongside appropriately scoped paid industry work. Clearly distinguish sponsors, study purposes, publication policy, potential conflicts and permitted derivatives; participant opt-in should differentiate academic vs commercial research and internal model improvement as already specified. No exclusive relationship should suppress scientifically relevant negative evidence or prevent appropriate benefit to users; negotiate intellectual-property, publication and model-reuse rights case by case without assuming blanket rights. Reasonable commercial sustainability can support research but is not a justification for data brokerage.

**Success measures:** defensible replicable findings, quality and representativeness of outcomes/measurements, useful validated actions and knowledge coverage, study transparency and participant trust; not number of identifiable records sold, amount of private health data accumulated or unsupported promises of increased lifespan. Bloom cannot promise individual longevity gains absent appropriate evidence.

**Scope unchanged:** This is a research-business design principle, **not** live authorization to pool personal data, recruit users, grant a partner access, sell datasets, run cohort training or introduce a research consent UI. Current private personal learning and existing Food/Response/science work continue; future research launch retains separate owner strategic authorization plus applicable consent, legal/security and ethics review.

## Decision and product intent
Preserve a realistic future capability to enable **voluntary academic contribution and commercial research/pharma partnerships**, including governed cohort analysis, reproducible scientific modeling and privacy-safe aggregate research outputs **without selling unrestricted individual health records**. Design for credible longitudinal exposure → repeated outcome evidence: diet/food form/dose, activity and intensity, sleep, actual versus planned medications/supplements, measurements/microbiome methods and individual context. Avoid labeling observational correlations as proven effects. Build only useful current personal tracking, canonical provenance and reversible source references; defer research participation UX, governance/IRB and cohort processing until separately authorized.

## Future-proof contracts: minimum technical properties now
1. **Private observation layer stays canonical**: original source values, analyte/nutrient and organism identifiers, units/form/method/specimen, provider release, collection/occurrence time and entered/revised time, time zone, measurement uncertainty, source revision and purpose-scoped consent; preserve reported vs observed vs estimated vs planned vs performed. Unknown ≠ zero. Record method comparability and repeated measures without inventing source detail.
2. **Intervention/exposure event vocabulary**: versioned start/stop/time windows, amount, food portion/preparation, activity type/intensity, sleep, supplement product+ingredient+form, adherence/recall uncertainty, contemporaneous confounders, adverse/neutral outcomes and one-click user feedback; no compulsory research survey, baseline/washout false precision or passive dose inference.
3. **Typed identifier compatibility**: use existing canonical source IDs and stable namespaces (FDC/CNF, LOINC, UCUM, SNP/build/allele, species/strain/taxonomy, Rhea/ChEBI, intervention action/rule versions), link original versioned records to derived snapshots and correction receipts. Future mappings to FHIR/LOINC/UCUM/OMOP/HL7 or other standards are **export compatibility**, not claims of certified interoperable implementation.
4. **Processing and jurisdiction metadata separate from user medical facts**: account residency or applicable legal region (self-declared/verified only as justified), source jurisdiction, storage/processor region, purpose, recipients, lawful processing basis or future consent grant ID, data class, retention policy, transfer/contract basis, model output status, study protocol/ethics version if applicable. Do not infer regulatory jurisdiction solely from IP or app locale.
5. **Versioned feature lineage**: derived summaries must link to exact original observation IDs/revisions, schema/normalizer, feature definition, evidence/model version, eligible time window, missingness and sensitivity category. Late corrections, withdrawal/deletion and method changes invalidate derived state and halt future secondary processing. Never let retroactive fixes silently rewrite historical analyzed snapshots.
6. **Separate future research boundary**: design for distinct policy-controlled export/contribution service, separate physical/logical storage and encryption/access scope, study/purpose authorization, recipient/contract rights, query/small-cell controls, monitoring, no super-user row access and no public raw genomic/biome extracts. This is a *future interface definition*, not authorization to create a production shared dataset or collect future research consent now.
7. **Distinct permissions in future design**: private personalized service; optional Bloom model-improvement participation; optional noncommercial academic research; optional commercial sponsored analysis; third-party identifiable data linkage; sensitive genetics/microbiome subtypes; cross-border data transfer; contact/re-contact; independent revocation. Do not bundle these or infer agreement from app use, generic privacy policy or `Show development features`; **do not present these choices until a separately approved real program exists**. User can use core Bloom without contributing.
8. **Withdrawal/erasure realism**: mark which original, derived, model and released aggregate representations are individually reversible; stop new processing promptly, remove/limit retained personal contribution where feasible/lawful, and transparently disclose that genuinely anonymous published aggregate results or trained models may not be fully retractable. Treat model membership inference, reidentification and rare-genotype risk as serious.
9. **Scientific quality and dataset commercial utility**: denominator for actual eligible cohort and consent by purpose, repeat-outcome timing, capture intensity, intake/day completeness, comparable assay/provider/microbiome platforms, selection and attrition bias, intervention adherence, confounders and participant-level train/test partition. No fake causal claim from before/after or size of dataset, no personal optimization promise from genotype/taxon alone.
10. **Business and academic outputs as distinct governed products**: (A) secure aggregate descriptive study; (B) controlled analysis-to-answer (customer submits protocol, gets disclosure-reviewed results); (C) study-specific, ethics/legal/permission-approved limited dataset where genuinely necessary; (D) separately validated models/research services. Prefer first two; no general searchable database of participants, no default identifiable data sales.

## Acceptance fixtures
1. Canadian account A vs US account B: all account observation, derived, cache and future-purpose metadata segregated; no cross-account SQL/API, job, search or model-training joins.
2. User declines future research: ordinary personal guidance continues with no degradation or research participation nudge; research data absent.
3. Distinct purposes academic vs commercial vs internal model improvement cannot be conflated, fabricated as granted or grandfathered through a generic service consent.
4. Canadian source lab with unit/assay version and US source lab with different intervals: native report remains truth; canonical comparability qualifies or abstains; no universal ranges.
5. Food data CNF and USDA market variants are source-pinned; old consumed meal unchanged on new provider release.
6. Genetic and microbiome source ids do not become personally measured enzyme flux, clinical diagnoses or exact concentrations.
7. Revoked/deleted source invalidates per-user derived analyses and any *future* participation eligibility; no unconsented material replicated.
8. A future synthetic authorized cohort with rare genotype+region+condition triggers disclosure rejection and small-cell suppression; no raw row retrieval.
9. Future aggregate training test uses participant-level holdout, missingness/confounders, model extraction audit and representative measurement strata; no causation inferred.
10. Law-region switch tests Canada, US and EU mock policies with unapproved purposes denied and explicit external-review gates; no blanket one-size-fits-all enabled flag.
11. Research dataset manifest has data-source/rights/transform, dates and separate `specified / acquired / rights cleared / science reviewed / implemented / tested / deployed / participant authorized`; no source version/date invented.
12. Evidence/AI reviewer cannot autonomously promote an individual’s observational data into clinical population recommendation or license a research dataset.
