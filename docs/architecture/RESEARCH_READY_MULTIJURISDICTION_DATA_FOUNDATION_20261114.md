# Bloom — Research-Ready, Multi-Jurisdiction Longitudinal Data Foundation
**Date:** 2026-11-14 | **Architect: APPROVE DESIGN-FOR-FUTURE, NOT ACTIVATE RESEARCH SHARING**
**Target countries:** Canada and United States from first architecture decisions; Europe/EU/EEA as future-ready optional market (UK and Switzerland are separate legal regimes).
**Controlling scope:** Existing `V06_HANDOFF.md` §20 and `PRIVACY_PRESERVING_LONGITUDINAL_AND_POPULATION_LEARNING_DECISION_20261114.md` prohibit current research network launch, recruitment, uncontrolled cross-account training, broad extracts or silent consent reuse. This *readiness* specification does not change that. Owner remains strategy decision authority; no routine owner scientific-review checkpoint.

## Decision and product intent
Preserve a realistic future capability to enable **voluntary academic contribution and commercial research/pharma partnerships**, including governed cohort analysis, reproducible scientific modeling and privacy-safe aggregate research outputs **without selling unrestricted individual health records**. Design for credible longitudinal exposure → repeated outcome evidence: diet/food form/dose, activity and intensity, sleep, actual versus planned medications/supplements, measurements/microbiome methods and individual context. Avoid labeling observational correlations as proven effects. Build only useful current personal tracking, canonical provenance and reversible source references; defer research participation UX, governance/IRB and cohort processing until separately authorized.

## Future-proof contracts: minimum technical properties now
1. **Private observation layer stays canonical**: original source values, analyte/nutrient and organism identifiers, units/form/method/specimen, provider release, collection/occurrence time and entered/revised time, time zone, measurement uncertainty, source revision and purpose-scoped consent; preserve reported vs observed vs estimated vs planned vs performed. Unknown ≠ zero. Record method comparability and repeated measures without inventing source detail.
2. **Intervention/exposure event vocabulary**: versioned start/stop/time windows, amount, food portion/preparation, activity type/intensity, sleep, supplement product+ingredient+form, adherence/recall uncertainty, contemporaneous confounders, adverse/neutral outcomes and one-click user feedback; no compulsory research survey, baseline/washout false precision or passive dose inference.
3. **Typed identifier compatibility**: use existing canonical source IDs and stable namespaces (FDC/CNF, LOINC, UCUM, SNP/build/allele, species/strain/taxonomy, Rhea/ChEBI, intervention action/rule versions), link original versioned records to derived snapshots and correction receipts. Future mappings to FHIR/LOINC/UCUM/OMOP/HL7 or other standards are **export compatibility**, not claims of certified interoperable implementation.
4. **Processing and jurisdiction metadata separate from user medical facts**: account residency or applicable legal region (self-declared/verified only as justified), source jurisdiction, storage/processor region, purpose, recipients, lawful processing basis or future consent grant ID, data class, retention policy, transfer/contract basis, model output status, study protocol/ethics version if applicable. Do not infer regulatory jurisdiction solely from IP or app locale.
5. **Versioned feature lineage**: derived summaries must link to exact original observation IDs/revisions, schema/normalizer, feature definition, evidence/model version, eligible time window, missingness and sensitivity category. Late corrections, withdrawal/deletion and method changes invalidate derived state and halt future secondary processing. Never let retroactive fixes silently rewrite historical analyzed snapshots.
6. **Separate future research boundary**: design for distinct policy-controlled export/contribution service, separate physical/logical storage and encryption/access scope, study/purpose authorization, recipient/contract rights, query/small-cell controls, monitoring, no super-user row access and no public raw genomic/biome extracts. This is a *future interface definition*, not authorization to create a production shared dataset or collect future research consent now.
7. **Distinct permissions in future design**: private personalized service; optional Bloom model-improvement participation; optional noncommercial academic research; optional commercial sponsored analysis; third-party identifiable data linkage; sensitive genetics/microbiome subtypes; cross-border data transfer; contact/re-contact; independent revocation. Do not bundle these or infer agreement from app use, generic privacy policy or `Show development features`; **do not present these choices until a separately approved real program exists**. User can use core Bloom without contributing.
8. **Withdrawal/erasure realism**: mark which original, derived, model and released aggregate representations are individually reversible; stop new processing promptly, remove/limit retained personal contribution where feasible/lawful, and transparently disclose that genuinely anonymous published aggregate results or trained models may not be fully retractable. Treat model membership inference, reidentification and rare-genotype risk as serious.
9. **Scientific quality and dataset commercial utility**: denominator for actual eligible cohort and consent by purpose, repeat-outcome timing, capture intensity, intake/day completeness, comparable assay/provider/microbiome platforms, selection and attrition bias, intervention adherence, confounders and participant-level train/test partition. No fake causal claim from before/after or size of dataset, no personal optimization promise from genotype/taxon alone.
10. **Business and academic outputs as distinct governed products**: (A) secure aggregate descriptive study; (B) controlled analysis-to-answer (customer submits protocol, gets disclosure-reviewed results); (C) study-specific, ethics/legal/permission-approved limited dataset where genuinely necessary; (D) separately validated models/research services. Prefer first two; no general searchable database of participants, no default identifiable data sales.

## Codex execution requirements — no competing project or extra gate
**P0 now (extensions only):** Audit current lab/food/genetics/microbiome/measurements/activity/supplement data for versioned input identity, method, repeat-observation and source correction; enforce existing user/purpose isolation; add missing nullable provenance/event fields only when justified by personal functionality (Issue #12/#15 and V06-13). No secondary research purpose or speculative PII retention. Produce explicit schema/compatibility map and targeted two-account privacy tests.
**P1 now:** Improve within-account longitudinal chosen→performed→outcome and unknown coverage, provenance, method comparability, user correction and revocation; add generic jurisdiction/purpose vocabulary only if needed for actual privacy/consent/security contract. Keep zero existing cross-user research queries.
**P2 design only:** Maintain documented future interface for separately consented research contributor eligibility, controlled deidentified/aggregate study views, permissible purpose/recipient, regional isolation/transfer and model lineage. No account-spanning data warehouse, joins, researcher portal, ads/data brokerage, pharmacovigilance reporting or consent UX activation by this decision.
**Activation in later separately approved phase:** owner specifies Canada/US/EU markets and noncommercial/commercial purposes, with Legal/Security/Research Ethics/Scientific/independent User Testing, validated consent & controls, regional deployment/transfer and purpose-specific data use agreements. Only then can a selected pilot cohort be built. Super-user research view does not unlock this.

## Acceptance fixtures
1. Canadian account A vs US account B: all account observation, derived, cache and future-purpose metadata segregated; no cross-account SQL/API, job, search or model-training joins.
2. User declines future research: ordinary personal guidance continues with no degradation or research participation nudge; research data absent.
3. Distinct purposes academic vs commercial vs internal model improvement cannot be conflated, fabricated as granted or grandfathered through a generic service consent.
4. Canadian source lab with unit/assay version and US source lab with different intervals: native report remains truth; canonical comparability qualifies or abstains; no universal ranges.
5. Food data CNF and USDA market variants are source-pinned; old consumed meal unchanged on new provider release.
6. Genetic and microbiome source ids do not become personally measured enzyme flux, clinical diagnoses or exact concentrations.
7. Revoked/deleted source invalidates per-user derived analyses and any *future* participation eligibility; no unconsented material replicated.
8. A future synthetic authorized cohort with rare genotype+region+condition triggers disclosure rejection and small-cell suppression; no raw row retrieval.
9. Future aggregate training test uses participant-level holdout, missingness/confounders, model extraction audit and representative measurement strata; no causation inferred.
10. Law-region switch tests Canada, US and EU mock policies with unapproved purposes denied and explicit external-review gates; no blanket one-size-fits-all enabled flag.
11. Research dataset manifest has data-source/rights/transform, dates and separate `specified / acquired / rights cleared / science reviewed / implemented / tested / deployed / participant authorized`; no source version/date invented.
12. Evidence/AI reviewer cannot autonomously promote an individual’s observational data into clinical population recommendation or license a research dataset.
