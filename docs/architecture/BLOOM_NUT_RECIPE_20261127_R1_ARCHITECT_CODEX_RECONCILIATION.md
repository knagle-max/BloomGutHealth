# BLOOM-NUT-RECIPE-20261127-R1 — canonical recipe-to-food-journal product and implementation directive

**Owner product direction:** November 27, 2026. **Disposition:** approved core product direction with architectural/scientific safeguards. **Owner's explicit clarification (same date) governs prioritization:** Bloom must provide a **comprehensive practical recipe library** in which a user **follows a real recipe** and then **logs the entire consumed recipe portion in one action**, automatically entering **every included ingredient and all source-supported nutrients** into the existing canonical food journal. Never require the user to rebuild a known recipe by individually logging each ingredient. This is the **primary consumer outcome**, not a distant optional feature that waits for full microbiome/genetics/AI modelling.

## 1. Required end-to-end user journey (binding)

1. **Discover and cook:** Browse/search/filter genuinely distinct, practical Bloom-curated, lawful-source or user-owned recipes. Each recipe shows familiar title, actual ingredient list with quantities/units, servings, equipment/time, cooking steps, accessible prep instructions and concise nutrition summary.
2. **Ingredient-linked data:** Ingredients resolve against the **existing** canonical food/source registry, with correct preparation, quantities, edible weights, nutrient identities, source version and per-nutrient known/unknown coverage. A recipe is not just a photo, unstructured text or generic calorie estimate.
3. **Adjust actual batch:** User can review/change the actual quantity, substitution, omission, cooked yield or serving count. The authoritative deterministic calculator recalculates nutrient contributions. Missing oil/salt/portion/source stays unknown or explicitly partial; do not fabricate.
4. **Log what was actually eaten:** A clear **Log meal** (or equivalent) action opens a compact confirmation of the **portion actually consumed** (e.g. 0.5, 1, 1.5 servings or measured grams), meal timestamp/type and any confirmed substitutions. On explicit confirmation, **one atomic/idempotent save** creates the existing canonical meal/consumption record with **all included original ingredient identities, quantities, source-grounded per-nutrient contributions and total/per-nutrient coverage**, linked to the recipe revision. **Do not make the user log every ingredient manually.**
5. **Everything updates:** After save, existing Food journal, Today's confirmed intake, recorded nutrient totals/coverage, Progress and eligible biological context use the **same canonical record**, not a parallel recipe ledger or independently computed totals. Planned/favourited/prepared recipes remain **not consumed**.
6. **Repeat easily:** Recent/favourite or personally edited recipe can be used again and logged quickly with last confirmed portion; still require explicit consumption confirmation. Editing the template later must **not** mutate past meals; correction/deletion use existing canonical revision and source semantics.
7. **Offline/AI-disabled core:** Recipe catalogue, ingredients, deterministic nutrition calculations, preparation instructions and one-action logging must work **without a paid recipe API or external AI**. AI proposals are optional and cannot invent composition or save food.

**Example acceptance flow:** a four-serving chicken gnocchi soup with known chicken, gnocchi, vegetables and broth. After cooking, the user confirms **1.5 servings** (or measured prepared grams if actual batch yield and allocation are supportable). Bloom creates **one canonical consumption event** containing the **0.375 batch fraction** of every included source-resolved ingredient and each compatible, available nutrient—not four separate manual logging interactions. If some seasonings/oil are unmeasured, show known nutrient subtotals as **partial**, not false complete zeros. Source dates/units/calculation version and the exact original recipe revision remain attached. The sum must match logging equivalent individual ingredients with the same identities, weights, source definitions and calculation version within explicit rounding tolerance.

## 5. Owner clarification — flexible weekly meal plans, pantry and dynamic groceries (2026-11-27)

**Binding owner product addition.** The recipe engine must power a **practical, editable seven-day meal plan** with optional AI suggestions, integrated grocery list, optional pantry/on-hand inventory and flexible changes when real life intervenes. This is a **first-class consumer workflow alongside one-action full-recipe logging**, not a separate planner/recommendation/inventory-calculation engine or a demand to complete advanced biology before meal planning works. Use existing `shared/weekly-planner.ts`, `shared/today-planner.ts`, `shared/food-preference-policy.ts`, `server/planner-routes.ts` and canonical dietary preference and journal records. Code inspection confirms existing `plannedMealChoiceSchema` (recipeId + portion), user dietary preference handling, basic locked plan choices, `recipeNutrients`, candidate filtering and metered AI proposal paths; **not** proof of persisted personal pantry, grocery reconciliation, calendar moves, skips or UI acceptance. Inspect current HEAD/schema/tests and reuse before extending.

### Required weekly planning journey

1. **Set week and constraints:** Choose a local calendar week, meal slots (e.g. dinners only or all meals), intended servings/household, available cooking time/equipment, budget if known, desired leftovers, dietary goals and existing **user-confirmed** household preferences. Personal preferences may differ by diner: show a review/household conflict rather than assume every family member shares an individual's restrictions. Plan and actual-consumption date/time remain separate.
2. **Generate practical suggestions:** Deterministic existing eligible recipe candidates remain available with AI completely disabled. Optional **AI-assisted** generation/re-ranking can propose practical variety, family preferences, available ingredients, preparation time and nutrient coverage, but must resolve each suggestion to an approved/validated recipe+ingredient identity and rerun the canonical allergy/exclusion/composition rules before display or save. Never treat model-invented ingredient weights, dietary safety or nutritional claims as verified. The user can manually add recipes or their own meals without AI.
3. **Editable weekly calendar:** Each slot has stable plan-entry ID, recipe ID and version, portion/household servings, local date/slot, `planned`/`skipped`/`replaced`/`prepared`/`completed` **with explicitly defined semantics** and optional locked status. Prefer event/revision-safe state consistent with current schema rather than enumerating new DB states blindly. **Move** to another date or slot, **swap** with another planned meal, **skip** without recording consumption, **replace** with an eligible recipe, **lock** a meal against regeneration, **regenerate only unlocked slots**, or mark a plan as a leftover. Moves/swaps must be atomic and not duplicate entries; conflicts, edits and account switching follow current optimistic revision/permissions. Skipping is not deletion of logged food; a logged meal keeps its original actual event date even if the original calendar slot moves.
4. **Pantry / groceries on hand (optional):** User can record food identity (including brand/preparation where relevant), approximate or precise quantity, unit/portion conversion, location, purchase/expiration date if known and whether **actually available**. Unknown quantities and uncertain identity remain visibly uncertain. A light **“I already have these”** flow from an editable grocery list can be used without requiring a perpetual inventory system; pantry must remain optional. Do not infer stock from past purchases, scanned labels, planned meals or logged consumption; actual on-hand and consumed are separate. Avoid a second food identity registry.
5. **Grocery list from current plan:** Expand the current **non-skipped** meal schedule into each recipe's ingredient list; multiply by scheduled household servings/actual recipe version; aggregate only matching compatible source ingredient identities and quantities, preserving purchase vs edible vs cooked form and package size. Apply user-confirmed pantry quantities only when compatible and sufficiently known, and show `need`, `have`, `still to buy` and `unknown` distinctly. Optional garnishes or unmeasured items become review entries, not invented zeros. Group by useful store/category, allow checked/purchased/manual items and manual adjustment. **Move same meal between days must not change total grocery demand**, while skip/replace/serving adjustment must deterministically recalculate it; preserve independent manual grocery entries and avoid silent deletion of already purchased items.
6. **Cook and log:** A planned meal opens its real recipe and instructions; once the user explicitly confirms what was actually eaten and portion, save via the **existing canonical journal** with **every included source-resolved ingredient and its supported nutrient contribution**. A `completed` label on the calendar is not authority to manufacture a food journal entry; distinguish `prepared` versus `actually consumed`. If the user eats something different, record the actual meal without falsely rewriting the original plan.
7. **Practical week changes:** Support “Tuesday ran late—move pasta to Thursday, use frozen pizza tonight”, “skip Wednesday”, “make tonight a leftover dinner”, “regenerate remaining unlocked dinners” and “use my chicken, gnocchi and frozen corn before buying more”, with visible and reversible changes to the planned week and recomputed grocery needs. AI may parse the request **into a reviewable proposal**, but must not silently rewrite confirmed plan records or mark any consumption. A pantry item expiring soon may influence ranking only if its date/status was actually provided; never guarantee food safety from an expiry date alone.

## 6. NRE-A01 culinary diversity and practical cooking amendment — 2026-11-27

**Owner's binding product amendment, `NRE-A01 — Culinary Diversity and Practical Recipe Intelligence`.** This is a *named amendment* to the whole `BLOOM-NUT-RECIPE-20261127-R1` product contract, **not** the already-defined acceptance scenario `NRE-A01` (deterministic USDA source import) in the parent handoff. For unambiguous implementation verification, identify new culinary tests as **`NRE-CUL-01..08`** while retaining the owner's amendment title exactly.

**Culinary-first rule:** Bloom must be a **genuinely useful cookbook and flexible family meal planner**, not 2,000 reclassified variations of chicken/rice/vegetables, a set of mathematical meal combinations, or nutrition optimization at the expense of flavour. A recipe is valuable if people can actually cook and enjoy it, with normal seasonings, fats, aromatics, sauces, garnishes, good instructions and realistic household quantities. A variation or personal substitution must preserve its relationship to the parent recipe and must **not count** as a new genuinely distinct catalogue recipe merely to inflate counts. Nutrition science enriches the actual meal; it must not replace culinary quality.

### A. Multi-dimensional recipe identity, filtering and coverage

Extend the **existing** recipe schema/catalogue/search and `shared/weekly-planner.ts` rather than separate appliance, cooking or category engines. Classifications are **independent many-to-many facets** and should be combinable:
- **Meal occasion:** breakfast, brunch, lunch, dinner, snack, dessert, side, appetizer.
- **Dish form:** soup, stew, salad, sandwich, wrap, pasta, noodles, grain bowl, casserole, curry, stir-fry, pizza, flatbread, burger, smoothie, baking, sauce, marinade, dressing, dip, spread.
- **Supported appliances and methods:** **Instant Pot (explicit visible first-class search/filter label)**, electric pressure cooker, traditional pressure cooker, slow cooker, air fryer, oven conventional/convection, stovetop, BBQ/grill, sheet pan, one pot, microwave, steam, no-cook. An Instant Pot may belong to the electric-pressure-cooker family, but **must not be hidden exclusively under that generic family**; a recipe may have multiple independently valid method profiles.
- **Practicality:** under 15/30/60 minutes **based on realistic total elapsed cook-to-table time**, family/kid friendly, budget, few ingredients, meal prep, batch, freezer-friendly, leftovers, entertaining/special occasion.
- **Cuisine, region and flavour:** genuine different culinary traditions, spices, aromatics, ingredient combinations, technique and optional user-set spice/heat level.
- **Supported nutrition/biology:** existing nutrient, dietary preference, training and scientifically accepted microbiome/biological relationship filters; no unjustified nutrient/clinical claims.

**Do not treat tags as complete proof:** A “30 minutes” recipe must fit the stated **total realistic elapsed time**, including passive pressure preheat/release, handling and finishing, not only pressure programme minutes. Some appliances are optional alternatives, not identical cooking scripts.

### B. Method-specific, safe preparation data

Provide a **method-specific preparation profile** for each actually supported appliance, with preparation duration, appliance settings, cooking steps, actual finishing and total elapsed time where qualified:
- **Instant Pot/electric pressure:** ingredient prep, pressure-build interval, pressure programme duration, **natural versus quick release and expected release time**, optional sauté/finishing, overall estimate; distinguish capacity and liquid/recipe-specific practical limitations.
- **Slow cooker:** high and low cook times and relevant food-prep requirements, actual end-to-end elapsed duration.
- **Air fryer:** temperature, duration, capacity/batch size, turning/preheating where needed, realistic handling.
- **Other methods:** conventional oven/convection, stovetop, grill, microwave, steam etc. with distinct validated steps when truly supported, not mechanically relabelled copies.
- Maintain food-safety guidance, source-specific raw/cooked edible weights, absorption/drainage, **method-appropriate yield/retention only when qualified** and no double-retention factors. “Recipe can be cooked in appliance X” is a reviewable claim, not a generated tag inferred from similar ingredients.

**Quality baseline for every curated entry:** culinary-distinct identity and version, realistic ingredient amounts, flavours/seasonings/fats, plausible preparation sequence, proper applicable equipment and timings, actual servings and yield assumptions, source-backed calculated nutrients with **missing values labelled**, safety guidance where needed, and legal/attribution status. If an amount is “to taste” or unmeasured, keep it editable/unknown rather than inventing precise nutritional quantities.

### C. Personalization and weekly integration

Reuse canonical user/household preferences for **available appliances, preparation time, servings, cuisine/flavour/spice, budget/leftovers and dietary constraints**. Apply these to **every** deterministic recipe search, Today/weekly suggestion, AI proposal, regenerated unlocked slot and ingredient substitution, with allergies and medically relevant exclusions enforced independently of soft tastes. Explicit “Instant Pot” search/filter and combined `dinner + Instant Pot + ≤30 minutes + family friendly + dietary restriction + supported nutrient criterion` must return only actually eligible recipes, or an honest no-match state.

Grocery lists, pantry-compatible quantity subtraction, leftovers, skip/move/lock and one-action **confirmed whole-recipe ingredient/nutrient journal save** must remain consistent with the selected recipe revision and cooking profile. A cooking-method change must re-evaluate any supported calculated yield/nutrient interpretation, ingredient changes and total time. Optional AI may suggest creative original recipes or variations, **but** proposed titles/tags/cook times/nutrients are not accepted until structured recipe, culinary viability, qualified composition, allergy and practical method validation; core diverse cookbook, search and planning work with AI off.

### D. Catalogue quality and diversity acceptance

Catalogue objectives of ~100, ~500 and eventually ~1,000–2,000 are **conditional quality goals**, not record-count release gates. Measure genuine recipe diversity across occasion, dish form, technique/appliance, regional flavour, time, ingredient families, household needs and seasonality. Keep `recipe_id`, `parent_recipe_id` and variant lineage distinct as supported by current schema/migrations; do not force new persistence fields before inspecting actual database. Candidate de-duplication should examine substantive technique, ingredient ratios, preparation and finished dish identity, not just titles/IDs. Include human culinary usability assessment on a representative selection; automated coverage cannot alone verify that a meal tastes good.
