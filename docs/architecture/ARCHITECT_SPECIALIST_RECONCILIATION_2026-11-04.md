## Binding architecture decisions

1. Reuse current versioned evidence/claim/rule registry and entity/source IDs. Do not build competing microbe, molecular, recommendation or imagery databases.
2. Present source observations verbatim and keep missing, unknown and zero distinct. A qualitative taxon report does not establish species fraction, strain toxin or measured enzyme activity.
3. Show useful microbial profiles now with truthful neutral artwork where individual morphology or rights cannot be substantiated. Individual scientifically reviewed portraits, molecules and pathways remain required staged deliverables, **not optional decoration**.
4. Chemistry SVGs must show specific verified identity. A four-circle motif is not a butyrate structure; butyric acid and butyrate require different formula/charge drawings.
5. Run action-oriented suggestions with evidence grading and absolute food exclusions first; molecular exploration remains progressive and cannot justify unsupported clinical personalization.
6. For food variety, count canonical verified culinary identities, not USDA item IDs, and do not treat the count as a validated outcome.
7. v0.5 remains browser/PWA-first, source-fidelity and working workflows first. Native HealthKit/Health Connect, paid data providers, quantitative microbial simulation, Coach and regulated research services follow separately approved scopes.
