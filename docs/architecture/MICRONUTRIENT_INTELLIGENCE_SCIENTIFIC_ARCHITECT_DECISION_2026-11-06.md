## 2. Binding product goal / non-negotiable truth boundaries
Users should be able to understand **what their actually recorded foods and confirmed supplements contribute**, **how much is known versus missing**, **which applicable reference is relevant**, and **which practical safe food choices are credible**. Rich micronutrient coverage remains in scope. Preserve distinctions:
1. `recorded_source_contribution` ≠ `estimated_usual_intake` ≠ `actual_biological_status` ≠ `diagnosed_deficiency`.
2. A source-confirmed **zero**, unknown composition, missing meal/day, unresolved amount/form, conflicting database sources and measured laboratory result are distinct typed states. Never silently zero-fill, fabricate completeness %, join incompatible units or treat unlogged days as eaten nothing.
3. Reference types **EAR / RDA / AI / UL / CDRR** are not interchangeable. RDA is a planning reference, UL a potential-risk reference **not a target**, below AI ≠ deficiency, absence of established UL ≠ unrestricted safety. Individual adequacy probability from sparse logs or universal `intake/EAR` division is unsupported. Menstruation-related iron requirements need special method where assessment truly available.
4. Vitamin/mineral chemical form and source matter: vitamin A retinol/carotenoid **RAE**; food folate/fortified folic acid and **DFE**; niacin/tryptophan **NE**; vitamin E forms and activity; vitamin D IU only when form/scale known; iodine from actual identified formulation; elemental vs compound iron/zinc/magnesium. Preserve source-native value and only derive equivalents when inputs support correct conversions. Unknown cannot be converted to exact RAE/DFE/NE/iodine/elemental amount.
5. **UL applicability is form/source and time dependent** (e.g. food vs non-food/supplement magnesium, natural folate vs synthetic folic acid, preformed vitamin A vs carotenoids, applicable niacin/supplemental forms). Do not apply generic UL to all foods/supplements or all age groups.
6. **Vitamin B6 reference divergence is material:** Biology cites Health Canada's October 2026 review of Canadian/NASEM/EFSA context and product warnings. Implement a versioned, authority- and formulation-aware safety disclosure; no simplistic green check at 10–15, 50 or 100 mg/day based solely on older 100 mg adult UL, no automatic toxicity diagnosis/dose change. These citations are claims from the Biology review; source authority and current legal implications must be checked at actual release, not used as blanket coding stop.
7. A scheduled or purchased **supplement is not consumed**. Include only confirmed dated ingestion or an explicitly verified recurring-consumption record in intake; preserve serving, label/formulation version, compound-versus-elemental dose and corrections, avoid duplicate contributions. This can be implemented with existing supplement/food events rather than a second intake store.
8. Food composition, quantitative lab biomarkers, genetic associations and qualitative microbial mechanisms must remain separate. No inferred absorbed iron percentage, personal B12/vitamin production, ferritin causality, dietary vitamin D→25(OH)D equation, genotype-specific dosing or organism-derived nutrient totals. Microbial vitamin pathways can be **developed and evaluated in the approved privileged/research shadow track**, not silently added to personal nutrient intake. Ordinary safe general food recommendations can proceed without genetics/microbiome.
9. Source rights/permissions: Biology identifies Canada Nutrient File (CNF) 2026 and Health Canada DRI tables; source availability does not itself prove commercial reuse, redistribution or brand-data equivalence. Reuse approved existing USDA/CNF provenance, confirm rights before distribution of any new corpus and never represent average CNF food value as an exact brand label.
10. Sensitive life-stage (child, pregnancy/lactation), medical/lab/genetic information and supplement use require least-necessary consent, server isolation, correction/revocation/deletion; no automatic therapeutic high-dose regimens or inapplicable adult references.

## 3. One canonical architecture; no second nutrition engine
Extend `nutrientDefinitions`, `shared/nutrition-summary.ts`, food catalog, canonical consumption events, existing lab provenance, data source/version and FoodProgress/NutritionHistory. **No parallel nutrition datastore, separate top-level micronutrient dashboard, second optimizer or new life-stage profile store** without demonstrable need.

Required typed extensions, following Biology's recommended names:
- `NutrientDefinition`: analyte ID, chemical form, activity/amount unit, exact source/form conversion basis, USDA/CNF mappings, version.
- `NutrientObservation`: confirmed dated food/fortified product/supplement ingestion, amounts/portions, form/elemental value where known, source and correction, `verified_zero | known | unknown | conflicting | estimated`.
- `DietaryReference`: authority, effective/version date, life-stage/pregnancy/lactation, EAR/RDA/AI/UL/CDRR kind, applicability to form/intake source and exception/method.
- `NutrientCoverage`: source-specific known subtotal and missing or uncertain contribution records; intake-recording window and reliability, **not** unsupported percent completeness.
- `IntakeAssessment`: observed intake separate from method-qualified usual intake, reference version, uncertainty/reason for abstention, source revision.
- `NutrientActionCandidate`: safe existing food/meal option, verified nutrient contribution, realistic portion, user allergy/preferences/macro/safety eligibility, reference/evidence and model/version. Python may propose candidate *within* eligible bounded options; existing deterministic policy rechecks. A suggestion isn't logged as eaten.

Preserve immutable source records and revisions across changes in database nutrient values, formulation labels and DRI version. Re-evaluation explains **reference/data revision**, not falsely “your body improved”. No huge new architecture or speculative migration.

## 5. Brand and user experience decision
Approve Branding's **recorded → coverage → reference → feasible action → optional deeper science** hierarchy in existing Today, Food Progress, Nutrition History, Explore and Biology. Plain-language first view:
- `Recorded from foods and confirmed supplements`, not `Your body has absorbed`.
- `This food has no verified iodine value`, not `0 iodine`.
- `Scheduled — not counted as taken`.
- `Recorded amount below planning reference` **only with adequate context**, not `43% deficient`.
- `Research model—not measured or counted as intake`, not `Your bacteria produced X mg`.

Approved minimal reusable patterns: `NutrientStatusRow`, `CoverageExplanation`, `ReferenceDisclosure`, `NutrientSourcesDrawer`, `SupplementConsumptionControl`, `FoodOptionComparison`, `ProfessionalReviewNotice`, `ResearchContextPanel`, but build only **demonstrated needed compositions** from existing components. No new full dashboard or red/green nutrient score, no pressure/deficiency alarm, no forced daily micronutrient action. Technical chemistry and original sources within two deliberate interactions. Sensitive age/life-stage questions are conditional and explain purpose. 320px/390px, 200% zoom, VoiceOver, keyboard and actual saved-data states required. Preserve established Everyday/Athlete design tokens.
