# Bloom — AI-First Visual Asset Production & Natural-Photography Prompt Standard
**Date:** 2026-11-14 | **Architect status:** APPROVED for current v0.6 app-wide premium theme implementation. Images/assets and hosted visual parity **not yet produced or verified** by this decision.
**Owner direction:** AI-generated imagery is an authorized **primary** source of Bloom application imagery; the owner should **not have to source or buy photography**. Follow the exact owner-supplied “Image Generation Prompt Rules (App UI, Photorealism & Anti-Plastic Enforcement)” from the 2026-11-14 chat, incorporated below with targeted implementation refinements. This **is not** permission to use arbitrary internet artwork, a request to add new app features, or evidence that generated images already exist.

## 1. Nonnegotiable aesthetic direction
Beautiful tactile editorial wellness photography within the established app-wide Bloom language: warm natural daylight, candid organic details, physically plausible food and human anatomy, real optical depth, purposeful negative space, and scientifically legible first-party botanical/anatomical artwork. Avoid the generic CG-looking, plastic-smooth, airbrushed, uniform-perfect style, anonymous stock-photo feeling, overprocessed saturation, artificial studio gloss and floating composited food. Harmonize:
- **Everyday:** botanical forest/sage, warm ivory, gold restraint, ceramic/linen/wood/light.
- **Athlete:** **existing authentic approved BLUE** accent, human movement and sport material texture; do not greenwash Athlete.
- **Coach:** **existing authentic approved PURPLE** accent, refined editorial professional/coaching imagery; do not greenwash Coach or misrepresent professional/patient relationships.
The three modes retain one optical, material, typographic and compositional family even with distinct accents.

## 2. Owner's mandatory realism rules + practical interpretation
For photographs or photography-like visuals, prompts must always specify **actual lens/camera/lighting cues and imperfect physical materials**, selected for the scene (not contradictory random presets): e.g., “35mm lens, f/2.8, natural film grain, directional side lighting from a window”, or for food macro “50mm lens, f/4, diffused daylight, physically plausible depth of field.” Use optics as visual guidance, **not fabricated EXIF provenance or an assertion a synthetic picture was shot on real equipment**.
Do **not** use the vague words “8k”, “hyperrealistic”, “ultra-detailed”, “masterpiece”, or “photorealistic” by itself as quality instructions. Prefer precise material, shape, environment, optical, lighting and composition guidance.
For services with separate negative prompts, start with the owner's negative sequence: `plastic skin, airbrushed, CGI, 3D render, smooth, wax, toy-like, oversaturated, deformed anatomy, artificial studio gloss, cluttered background, embedded text, watermarks`. **Context refinement:** Avoid blanket “smooth” or “wax” when accurate smooth yogurt/ceramic or natural waxy plant structures are intended; clarify instead `unnaturally uniform plastic skin / wax-figure gloss`. For stylized **scientific diagrams and vector illustrations**, “CGI/3D render” are not universal bans: choose a consistent illustration vocabulary. Negative prompts are not technically supported by every image service; where unavailable, integrate constraints as ordinary prompt language.
Never create fake medical screenshots, diagnostic images, device measurements, clinician verification or observed biological phenomena and label them factual. Scientific illustrations are *conceptual*; anatomy must be accurate if educational.

## 3. Role-aware framing, generated for their final use
| Intended surface | Format and direction | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| Wide hero/banner | `16:9` default, `21:9` on verified wide layouts | Subject anchored on the rule of thirds, **explicit clean negative space** for UI overlay on the side matching actual text placement, gentle environmental blur, natural optical depth; separate responsive crop/focal point, never embedded UI text |
| Editorial hero tile / split hero | `4:3` or `3:2` as actual component requires | One compelling focal subject, quiet ambient field for editorial heading nearby; keep image readability with source tested overlay |
| Standard feature accent | `1:1`, `4:3` or `4:5` as actual component needs | Centered or deliberately placed clear single focal point, high visual separation, real tactility, uncluttered backdrop, margin for crop |
| Food-only isolated asset | `1:1`, preferably genuine alpha when cutout requested | Fully visible single bowl/plate/ingredient, natural shadows within object, no utensils/props unless requested; photograph-style material accuracy and transparent-edge QC |
| Scientific figure/pathway | Source-linked editable SVG/diagram where appropriate, not a fabricated micrograph | Illustrated, labeled separately by app UI, pathway nodes accurate and evidence-status controlled; not pretending to be microscopic measured imaging |
| Athlete movement | `16:9` sports hero, `4:5` or `1:1` exercise accent | Accurate movement mechanics, realistic body diversity, not exaggerated strength, unretouched fabric and skin; equipment/hand/grip geometry credible |
| Coach contexts | Wide/portrait as designed | Credible real-world coaching interaction, inclusive people and professional environment, nonclinical by default; no implied doctor/clinic unless actually intended |

The component placement determines aspect, orientation, negative-space side and crop; **do not hardcode 35mm/f2.8** when a long-lens portrait or macro optics is appropriate. Never place important anatomy/food outside safe crops. Avoid baked-in labels, numbers, brand marks, buttons and watermarks; app renders accessible localized text.

## 4. Specific content injection requirements
**Food:** Organic irregular shapes, authentic edible preparation, moisture and temperature cues *only where physically appropriate* (steam on hot food, not chilled yogurt; oil sheen only on genuinely oiled food; crisp edges where roasted). Visible grain structures, crumb texture, real blueberry bloom/scars/seeds, realistic vessel/portion geometry, modest natural imperfections. Avoid impossibly uniform shape, missing ingredients or artificial food textures. Match the **actual** food item and ingredients/serving represented; never auto-show seafood for a user with seafood exclusions, or display a recommended meal image containing excluded allergens. Do not imply AI imagery is the user's real meal or proof they ate it.
**Athlete / anatomical humans:** Realistic skin pore texture and natural colour variations, subtle sweat only with suitable exertion, fine body hair when appropriate, directional muscle shadow and anatomy-accurate tendon/attachments, believable posture, clothing and equipment. Broad representation and dignity; no steroidized physique, deformed hands, extra fingers or impossible joint positions. If illustrating an exercise, pose must pass a movement-safety/anatomy sanity check; do not use decorative art as form instruction unless reviewed.
**Botanical/biological:** Accurate recognizable cell/tissue/plant morphology where used as instruction; distinguish metaphorical botanical art from histology or an actual laboratory result. Never suggest simulated SCFA production, blood markers or microbial concentrations are personally measured because a figure looks clinical.
**Coach:** Warm professional interaction, authentic real-world depth and credible accessories without identifiable clinic branding or patient confidentiality cues.
**All:** Stable palette, believable material contact, purposeful camera crop, no exaggerated saturation or fabricated scientific labels in pixels.

## 5. Recommended prompt contract (store alongside asset)
Each generated asset has: `assetId`, `surface`, `mode`, `subject`, `source factual constraints`, `asset role hero/accent/cutout/figure`, `target aspect/viewport/crop-safe-zone`, `requested dominant palette`, `camera lens/aperture/angle/light`, `physical materials/realism details`, `negative constraints`, `promptVersion`, `generation tool/model and allowed-use record`, `generation date`, `file digest`, `rights/usage review`, `QC status`, `approved filename or first-party URL`, `responsive focal point`, `alt-text` and optional scientific reviewer signoff if anatomy/biology educational. Avoid storing private genetic/lab/user prompts in external image tools without distinct permissions.
**Reusable prompt scaffolds** (the actual generator should fill non-contradictory scene specifics, not print tokens literally):
- **Hero photo:** “Editorial [accurate subject/action] in [physically plausible environment], natural [specific material] texture and organic imperfections; [chosen lens], [appropriate f-stop], daylight [direction], subtle natural grain; 16:9 environmental composition, main subject on [right] third, uncluttered low-contrast atmospheric [left] third reserved for UI heading; natural depth of field, believable object contact, subdued Bloom [mode] colour hints. No baked-in typography/logos/watermarks, plastic/CG gloss or unreal anatomy.”
- **Food accent:** “Single actual [dish with exact ingredients], authentic portion and food prep, irregular hydrated grain/vegetable surface and physically appropriate temperature/oil/steam, handmade ceramic, 50mm lens f/4, window daylight upper left, very subtle photographic grain, three-quarter tabletop angle; square 1:1 complete plate with crop margin, neutral minimal background, clear food fidelity, no unrelated props, embedded text, fake high gloss or uniformly perfect food.”
- **Athlete hero:** “Athlete performing [correct specific movement] in real [location], natural skin variation and pore texture, credible muscles/tendons/hand grip, lightly textured clothing and realistic sweat where appropriate, 35mm lens f/2.8, directional natural side lighting, subtle grain, 16:9 subject on right third leaving left-third negative UI text space, blue-led Bloom Athlete ambience without artificial blue skin, no unnatural anatomy, plastic-looking skin or text.”
- **Coach accent:** “Authentic collaborative [coach and adult participant] interaction around [real task], candid posture and natural skin/hair/fabric, 50mm lens f/2.8, soft side window light, restrained purple environmental styling, square 1:1 crop-safe composition with a clear focal point, accessible backgrounds, no branded monitors, embedded text or artificial gloss.”
- **Diagram:** “Original editorial scientific atlas illustration of [only vetted relationship], restrained thin vector-ink lines, botanical/scientific forms anatomically and biologically valid for chosen level of abstraction, clear reserved areas for accessible app-created labels, warm ivory base and mode accent, NOT a photo of an actual patient/micrograph or a measured personal physiological event.”

## 6. No owner photography procurement / production workflow
**Design specialist:** Provide a prioritized **scene/placement shot list** for the entire app by route, then prompt briefs/visual directions linked to the actual component and crop. **Codex:** use an authorized image-generation tool/workflow to create needed first-party artwork at sufficient resolution, or integrate the generated assets supplied by the design process; cannot pretend an unavailable image-generation API exists in its runtime. If developer lacks an approved image tool/capability, call out the exact asset-production blocker and continue implementable layout with a designed placeholder. Do **not** request that the owner source or license third-party photography. Avoid browsing images as an invented requirement.
Generate **small, coherent batches** (e.g., one hero and one accent from each key family) to calibrate style, then propagate. Repeatable prompts and file/source QC make later regeneration straightforward. Ensure images are eligible for application's intended commercial use according to the actual provider terms/rights and subject permissions; no claims of exclusive copyright or absolute likeness rights.
**Quality check** must include food factual matching, anatomy and movement correctness, cropping/safe zones and responsive usage, compression/responsive derivatives, text overlay legibility, dark-mode/blue/purple visual cohesion, alt-text, no disallowed ingredients or false real-world user photo claim, and loading/offline fallback. Preserve original assets and generated source + derivatives in the existing first-party asset registry; no broken external Stitch image links, no unexplained file bloat.
