## 4. Science, legal and product guardrails

- Evidence tier A/B/C/R is **not** intervention eligibility. Association, functional mechanism, assay-observed genotype and evidenced individual action must be distinct; preserve conflicting and null publications, population/ancestry limitations and review dates. No automated genotype-only supplement dose, exercise modality, disease prognosis or medication decision.
- Favor measured Lp(a), 25(OH)D, lipid, iron and other lab data over isolated candidate SNPs for direct clinical context. An absent LPA tag does not mean normal Lp(a). No assumption that a common marker predicts a deficiency or another user's lab result.
- APOE is consent-sensitive; genetic data is optional and domain education must be usable without upload. Preserve source correction/re-import history, deletion/retention controls and server-enforced scope. External scientific search never transmits user genotype by default.
- No publicly enabled high-stakes PGx or HLA diagnostic inference from research cards; no extra paid providers. Progress and Today may consume **only approved** actions, not raw exploratory genotype scores.
