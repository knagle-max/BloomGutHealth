## Controlling owner product purpose
Bloom is **research-informed personalized wellness**: users access current research, explore their own biological records and receive safe ordinary food, exercise and lifestyle suggestions relevant to their goals. Disease diagnosis, prevention/treatment advice, medical clearance and medication/hormone adjustment are not intended. **Do not automatically ban lab/omics-informed educational suggestions**; assess actual output, evidence and user decision under `INTENDED_USE_AND_CLAIMS_GOVERNANCE_DECISION_2026-10-30.md`, with independent Canada/US conclusions if implicated. No new commercial/regulated provider activation or paid service without owner authorization.
