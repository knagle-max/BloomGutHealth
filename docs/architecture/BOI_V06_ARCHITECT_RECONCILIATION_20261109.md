## Strategic acceptance
BOI is the **connecting intelligence across existing Today, Food, My Biology, Explore, Progress and Athlete**, not a new top-level destination, second source-of-truth, 0–100 biological efficiency number or expanded compulsory onboarding. Its guiding user questions: **what is working, what could improve, why does evidence support an action, and what did we actually observe afterwards?** The release-eligible v0.6 part is an independently reviewed *everyday wellness action* with personal context, explicit choice and honest follow-up. Separately: a cautiously evaluated individual response loop, and a serious *reference-only biological model R&D* track.

Keep as separate and visible: **recorded measured result; device estimate; provider assessment; population research association; hypothetical model scenario; unknown.** Never infer exact microbiome flux, individual GLP-1 secretion, genetic pathway efficiency, nutrient deficiency, diagnosis, treatment or contraindication-free medical action from taxa/SNPs/consumer devices.

## Scientific, data, security and legal non-negotiables
- Owner-consented actual user health/genetic data stays under canonical TypeScript/Postgres access control; private Python computations receive minimal opaque authorized inputs, no raw PDFs/DNA; inferential research never writes into measured source tables.
- Hard exclusion/contraindication and appropriate evidence/treatment restrictions before ranking; opt-out and no suitable action are valid outcomes; user priorities and workload burden visible.
- Snapshots/version/revisions prevent stale recommendations after edited portions, changed goals, source deletion, consent revocation or changed claim approval; preserve historical reality.
- Validate external reference licences at **dataset AND component/solver** level before commercial incorporation. Rhea/MetaNetX licences as reported by Integration are research leads, not a blanket combined commercial permission. AGORA2 availability not equal to rights or verified strain function.
- No native wearable connection until actual deployable bridge, auth and exact records tested. No inferred personal hormone response from CGM or microbiome. External paid provider activation is a feature-specific authorization issue, **not a stop-work instruction**.
- Independently verify R01–R17 and ML-cited study abstracts, methods, claim scopes and dates before any consequential user-facing copy. These handoffs are sources for *design requirements*, not independent certification of their empirical assertions.
