## 2. Preserve full original scientific chain, with a truth boundary at each edge
**Required research scope:** **taxon/species/strain → genome/gene → protein/enzyme (EC) → reaction (Rhea where appropriate) → substrate/cofactor/transport → produced AND consumed molecule/metabolite → organism/community exchange/cross-feeding → tissue/host compartment and possible host interaction → dietary substrate/food mapping → scenario comparison → theoretical objective optimization → separately evaluated human evidence.**

The objective is to estimate which biochemical *capacities or modelled fluxes* are possible under explicit assumptions, which molecules may be consumed/produced, how changing substrates or model members changes modeled results, what alternative food ingredients contain relevant substrates **where measured/composition-supported**, and how assumptions alter rankings. Include negative/infeasible cases and net production **minus consumption**, transport and missing-host-compartment limitations. Do not confuse gross producer count with net secretion or stool concentration.

These states MUST be typed and visible, not just a caveat added after a number:
- `source_observed_taxon`, `measured_abundance`, `measured_gene`, `measured_transcript`, `measured_protein`, `measured_metabolite`, `measured_flux` where source supports them;
- `published_strain_capacity`, `curated_pathway_possible`, `modelled_reaction_flux`, `modelled_community_exchange`, `hypothetical_food_substrate_scenario`, `human_association`, `human_intervention_effect`, `approved_everyday_action`, `clinical_claim_withheld`.
A qualitative Viome report lists organisms and categorical provider assessments; it does **not** measure relative abundance, metagenomic enzyme presence, actual production flux, faecal/blood concentration, toxin gene or personal clinical response. Species/strain reference mapping must not invent the specific person's strain/gene/expression. Missing/not reported is not zero.

**Initial reviewed model families:** acetate/lactate→butyrate cross-feeding, propionate, resistant starch and oligomeric fermentable substrates, mucin utilization, TMA (microbial)→TMAO (host FMO3) separate compartments, selected bile-acid conversions; include at least one falsifying/negative or ambiguous fixture per family. The examples are research programme seeds, not assured food mechanisms or automatic medical recommendations. No universal principle that more of any metabolite is healthier. Never turn a B. fragilis species listing into BFT status/toxin suppression or a human TMAO-treatment regimen.
