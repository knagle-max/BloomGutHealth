## 1. Binding owner-value and release-scope reconciliation

The Product Owner prefers **55 well-researched genuinely useful interpretations to 150 superficial descriptions**. **55 is an illustrative comparison, not a fixed scientific threshold, v0.5 acceptance minimum or new quota.** Likewise **3–5 top findings is a testable UX design default, not an exact number mandated by the owner**; allow zero, one, two or three if those alone qualify.

Architect decision under delegated scope authority:
- **KEEP in v0.5:** All specifically named Biology §5A candidate entities/types, 17 domains, original-source/29-item import and allele correctness, researched candidate catalogue, full source and educational browsing, source/version/consent controls. Do not silently omit the agreed named inventory or lose source observations.
- **RECLASSIFY the former 150–250 minimum:** aspirational **progressive research-catalogue coverage**, **NOT a v0.5 release-blocking requirement and NOT 150–250 user-specific interpretations**. Record the current count and shortfall transparently and extend with new credible reviewable evidence after higher-value completeness. This expressly supersedes prior lines that say 'no v0.5 closure while the 150–250 target is unmet'. A smaller thoroughly reviewed catalogue is acceptable **only with all explicitly named agreed candidate items retained**, safe empty/review-pending semantics and no false completeness claim.
- **NO interpretation-count quota.** Separate valid observed calls, scientifically reviewed genotype–phenotype educational statements, personally eligible highlights and clinical interventions. Promote an interpretation only after per-claim review and verified compatible genotype. No filler for catalogue targets or headline density.
- A lack of 55 reviewed claims does not fail v0.5 by itself. What matters is accurate source handling, core eligibility, useful exploration and honest reviewed content. This scope shift is an explicit Architect decision, not deletion of the owner's 17-domain/named-variant commitments.

## Chief Architect correction — eliminate over-gating of useful genetics education (2026-11-05)

**Binding clarification / supersession:** Prior "zero personal highlights until every claim qualifies" language is **too broad** if applied to all genetics content. Distinguish ordinary **observed genetic findings with sourced educational context** from **personal biological predictions and actionable/clinical claims**. Do not require a clinical/specialist signoff for each ordinary factual source card before any genetics homepage content can appear. This amendment controls wherever earlier sentences imply otherwise.

**Two clearly labelled surfaces within one existing Genetics experience, not two new engines:**
1. **Your genetic findings — source-backed education (launchable once technically verified).** Display valid directly observed alleles/variant calls with correct build/strand/type/status and the existing reviewed or source-cited **general** gene/variant research explanation. Use exact observation plus a scoped statement such as `This variant has been studied in relation to fatty-acid metabolism; the size and relevance of any effect for you are uncertain`. Highlight 0–5 useful cards if available, with `Why shown`, study references, source data and `See all`. If specific variant–phenotype association itself lacks sufficiently credible corroboration, limit to verified genotype plus general gene function and mark specific association `research under review`, rather than inventing one. A card can be personally **selected** due to the user's observed variant and goals without asserting a personally **predicted effect**. Verified measured genetic calls must not disappear into a zero-state while claim review proceeds.
2. **Personalized interpretations/actions (separate, strictly gated).** Claiming an individual has altered biological function, measured nutrient deficiency, increased disease risk, clinical diagnosis, treatment response, supplement dosage, expected exercise performance or causal lab effect requires independently reviewed applicable **variant–phenotype/clinical** evidence, assay sufficiency, consent and appropriate specialist/legal authorization. These claims remain hidden or neutral until genuinely eligible. Sensitive APOE, PGx, HLA, complex haplotypes, genotype-derived medical management remain strongly gated.

**No rubber-stamping:** Source citation alone is not proof of a specific allele's phenotypic effect. Biology review is required for *new consequential personal effect or intervention claims*, not as a general administrative approval on every factual observed call or established nonclinical biology description. Use already reviewed basic explanatory content; adding five educational cards should not trigger 55 new clinical signoffs, a new schema, a second engine or another giant catalogue fill exercise.

**UI states:** `Your genetic findings` shows source-observed result and carefully qualified sourced research; `Personalized interpretation` is a separately gated section/label and can truthfully show none. If zero supported interpretation claims exist, **only that subsection** shows none, not the entire Genetics overview. No upload => no personal calls, but general Explore remains. Keep 17-domain explorer, optional education, original 29 explanations and all valid measured calls. Preserve uncertainties and a clear disclaimer about limited clinical utility rather than generic “not qualified” everywhere.

**Engineering exit:** On the current branch inspect reuse of existing variant card component, import state and approved 29 explanations. Implement a working main Genetics overview showing at least one **synthetic-source** measured-variant card with correctly sourced, caveated education, plus See all and zero-gated-actions state; verify a real eligible user observation is shown only to its owner, missing/ambiguous calls never impersonate measured, citations correct, consent-off hides sensitive, account switching and deletion invalidate cached items. No actual personal genotype data in public Git or analytics. Do not invent clinical interpretation or require an external reviewer merely to unblock factual cards. Only claim-specific higher-stakes content waits.

**Acceptance classification:** Source-observed card and generally cited education = ordinary accuracy/UX acceptance; new unverified specific allele–outcome effect remains research-only; high-stakes personalized clinical/PGx/therapy claims = explicit claim review. Remove the blanket `zero personal highlights` policy where it suppresses safe observed facts; preserve a zero state for **unsafe or unsupported personal predictions**.
