## 1. Product Owner dynamic-data rule — overriding all exemplar counts

**ALL counts and food assignments must be generated from the actual user-selected saved report.** The 13-page reference handoff describes **299 source foods** (240 Enjoy, 28 Superfood, 22 Minimize, 9 Avoid), 22 categorical assessments, plus 94 organism entries in the companion report. This is **one source-specific extraction claim and fixture expectation**, not a fixed Viome specification, universal expected size, parser limit, user-visible default, ranking baseline or required minimum. Different users, report versions, dates, PDFs, corrections and partial extracts may contain different category counts, food names, categories and missing sections. Do not hardcode 299, 240/28/22/9, 22, 94 or the fixture oats/Superfood/bell pepper/Avoid examples anywhere in product decisions or copy.

**Dynamic contract:** At request/render, load only the current authenticated user's **explicitly selected, reviewed and saved** recommendation report version, taking the effective corrected category/label and current source status. Derive counts `total = validFoodRows.length` and per-category `count = validFoodRows.filter(row => row.effectiveCategory === category).length`, with any actual unmatched/needs-review rows reported separately (counts must be clearly labelled e.g. source-reported vs valid-for-influence). If no selected eligible source or zero food entries, show an honest empty state and keep ordinary Food working. The complete report remains visible while opt-out. No assumption omitted food means Avoid, missing = 0 or not recommended.

**Branding source conflict 299 vs 371:** The Branding handoff records an earlier **371-row** extraction claim versus **299** in the latest Biology/UT handoffs. Neither number should be silently “corrected” to match the other, used as a production validation hard-stop, or averaged/merged. **Reconcile by identifying the exact original document, document kind, source version, parser/fixture manifest and each row's page/column/category**, separating repeats, headings, description fragments and genuinely different reports. The supplied layout-faithful synthetic 13-page PDF is expected to have 299 **only after its manifest is independently verified**; an authentic different report legitimately yielding 371 must not be rejected for its count alone. Record a source discrepancy and partial-review state where verification fails, never fabricate or suppress rows. This is a **per-source correctness task** in the existing P0 Viome layout-aware PDF acceptance, not a new owner approval blocker.

## 2. Single account-level, default-Off opt-in

- **P0:** One visible account-scoped setting `Use my microbiome food recommendations`; **Off by default**, including new/migrated accounts and after import. Optional plain-language first-use explanation: `Let foods listed in your report help personalize meal ideas and food suggestions.` and `This won't change your nutrient totals or restrict what you can eat.` The user can toggle any time; permission affects only **in-app optional suggestions/ranking**, not report reading, logging, nutrients, historical records, treatment, external providers, analytics or new AI transmission. No second provider consent implied.
- Primary discovery: end of **successful report review/import** and `My Biology → Gut reports → Food suggestions`; persistent single toggle in `Profile → Food preferences` or existing analogous settings. Shared persisted account consent, not one toggle per screen or a forced wizard. Default is an Architect-selected safety default, **not misrepresented as a user-selected preference**.
- Report selection: if multiple report versions, the user explicitly selects the **governing report**, with non-identifying internal source IDs and verified biological dates/compatibility. No “latest auto-activate,” no merging conflicting same-named food rows, no linkage by retained sample/test/client IDs, DOB or identifying document fingerprint. The Viome strict PII exclusion overrides any Biology and Branding request to use private vendor identifiers for linking.
- Source correction, report deletion, opt-out, profile switch, consent revocation or report selection changes immediately invalidate server/client cached rank influence/badges and explanations. Logs, journals, independent nutrition and source history under authorized retention rules remain unaffected. An old report remains historical, not a statement of today's microbiome.

## 3. One deterministic source-to-food resolver and bounded influence

**P0 food identity:** Use canonical food/ingredient IDs from the existing food system; store original Viome label + exact report category, date/page/row, source version, match confidence and reviewed/corrected identity mapping. No second table of imported food “rules” disconnected from original record. `Almond Milk (Unsweetened)` ≠ almonds; `Kefir (Cow Milk)` ≠ plant kefir; `Yogurt (soy, plain)` ≠ dairy; `Green Onion (greens only)` ≠ whole onion; `Walnut Oil` ≠ walnuts; `Green Lentils` ≠ arbitrary lentil preparations. Unknown, preparation-unverified, partial and ambiguous matches are **no ranking signal** unless user explicitly reviews and validates the identity.

**Precedence:** (1) allergy/medical restriction and confirmed intolerance; (2) user exclusion/dislike, dietary pattern, ingredient requirements; (3) verified nutrition needs/goals and independent evidence; (4) opted-in source-provider food category as **modest secondary tie-breaker**; (5) variety, convenience, cost and existing ordering factors. A report's Enjoy/Superfood/Minimize/Avoid are **provider opinions** only, not allergies, medical exclusions, bacterial growth effects, measured metabolites, numeric scores or therapy. `Avoid`/ `Minimize` are soft contextual signals: no blocked logging, red warning, shaming, macro changes or blanket months-long avoidance. User-specific override/ignore survives source reimport and does not rewrite provider category.

**Across features:** One shared, versioned resolver integrated into only those existing surfaces which generate ranked choices: Food Discovery, Food of the Week, meal suggestions/planner, substitutions, supported remaining-budget options and recipes (P1 progressive by actual active surface). Ordinary food search, selection, diary logging and direct source browsing operate fully with Off/no source. No new food engine or dashboard.

**Recipe integrity:** Only confidently linked ingredients contribute, each with its own provider-reported category, correctly separated even if mixed categories or unmatched ingredients; never give a composite recipe an invented overall “Viome Superfood” grade. Swaps re-run eligibility without rewriting user logs or nutrient values.

## 4. Branding marker: material causal contribution, not mere category presence

Approve exact microcopy in the Branding source:
- Setting: `Use my microbiome food recommendations`
- Compact neutral outlined marker (not dominant card): `Microbiome report match`
- Details: `Your report was one factor in this suggestion.`
- Read-only report lookup when no rank influence: `Listed in your microbiome report`
- No match: `We couldn't match this food reliably, so it won't affect suggestions.`
- Off: `Report preferences are off. Your saved report is still available.`

**Critical architectural badge test:** The marker is shown only when the **report signal materially changed selection or ordered prominence** in a deterministic controlled **On vs Off counterfactual** with the same non-report inputs and stable tie-breaking (or equivalent traceable, reproducible evidence of decision contribution). Merely having the food listed in the report, signal enabled, report consulted, or passing a score into a routine is **not enough**. If two ranked results are identical and source did not change why the item appears, show no marker, even though source details remain accessible via `Listed in your microbiome report`. Store or compute a lightweight non-PII decision trace with source version, mapping/reason and contribution flag per result, without logging personal report content externally. Do not make marker globally sticky after cache invalidation.

**Why this suggestion?** one concise expandable disclosure: source-provider identification, exact original food name/category, source report date/page, why it was considered, other verified nutrition/preference goals, relevant confidence and user actions `Ignore this suggestion` / `Turn off report influence`. Do not show generic scary science warnings on every card. No provider name in compact icon, but provider visible in details and source report. Use plain-language semantics and accessibility; no invented named microbe effect/benefit.
