## Binding algorithmic/product contract — use existing pipeline

**One canonical route:** authenticated owned source/revisions/consent → scoped dated context → **reviewed action IDs and supporting evidence** → safety/contraindication/scope veto → opportunity evidence + missingness classification → candidate de-duplication → deterministic explainable relative ordering → action/alternative reasons + user choice → actual performed record → descriptive outcome feedback. Optional LLM/ML has no authority to generate new medically consequential candidates or override veto.

1. **Candidate coverage:** Draw a finite set of approved, concretely feasible candidates from diet/meal substitutions, protein/fibre/energy opportunities, movement, recovery, preferences/goals, and safe lab-related general actions. Do not require DNA, microbiome, device or complete logs. Do not invent deficits or interpret unreviewed markers as deficiencies.
2. **Hard veto first:** Verify owner consent, action-level age/condition/contraindication relevance where known, allergy/exclusion, applicability, evidence status, source currency, needed inputs and clinician-only classifications. Unknown contraindications are not silently 'safe'; choose appropriate general safe candidate, clarify relevant unknown, or abstain on that intervention.
3. **Separate dimensions:** `evidenceStrength` = quality/applicability of approved population-level research; `personalDataReliability` = provenance/completeness/assay/method/recency of user information; `goalRelevance`, `observedOpportunityEvidence`, `populationExpectedUtility`, `timeCost`, `effort`, `budget`, `acceptability`, `materialGoalTradeoffs` and `overlapGroups` retain distinct enumerated reasons. No invented personal benefit %, probability, recovery clock, BMR inference or arbitrary single optimization score presented to consumers. A formula may aid stable selection but is **not validated highest-impact science**.
4. **Fair across domains:** More measurements ≠ greater priority. Eligibility and calibrated evidence-based comparison are independent of how many records a domain emits; include a fair candidate consideration process for under-recorded domains and appropriately general actions. Sparse data should reduce attribution specificity, not default to deprioritization or deficiency.
5. **Group overlap:** Collapse substantially equivalent actions (e.g. 'walk after lunch' and 'increase daily walking') into one coherent suggestion with multiple supported goal benefits where applicable. Avoid duplicates among primary and alternatives; contraindications must still apply at composite-action level.
6. **Rank and explain:** One meaningful **primary** action and **up to three** distinct secondary opportunities, zero where nothing eligible. Explain *why now, why ahead of a competing opportunity, which recorded facts contributed, which evidence supports it, time/effort and uncertainty*, with explicitly labelled `general` vs `context-personalized`. Offer inspect/edit preferences, Try/Save Later/Not for Me; no selected-as-performed equivalence. Show useful actions before introducing hypothetical research paths.
7. **Information gain separately:** A data-gathering question is its own optional proposal and not mistaken for behavior change. Ask one question only if the answer would materially change eligibility or ordering, with explanation and `Unknown / Prefer not` options. Consequential clinical concern follows a reviewed clinician-discussion pathway **outside** routine diet/exercise scoring, not a 'highest wellness score' item.
8. **Recompute reproducibly:** Candidate decision includes authenticated user, local date/time context, source IDs/revisions, consent/goal/preference versions, approved action/evidence version, eligibility/reason codes, overlap/choice versions and deterministic tie-break. Corrections, deletes, revocations, profile/goal changes, evidence withdrawals or feedback changes invalidate affected ranked outputs; display no stale recommendation and protect cross-account reads, retry and version history.

## Release scenarios / mandatory scientific evaluation
- **Supported lifestyle vs speculative microbiome mechanism:** select reviewed feasible lifestyle action; microbial research can only appear as source-labelled education.
- **Multiple goals:** show credible conflict (e.g. protein vs fibre, energy vs fat quality) and why leading action wins while presenting a feasible alternative.
- **Limited food intake:** missing meals/composition remain unknown; no invented deficiency or intake balance.
- **Rich food, sparse movement:** a reasonable general movement action remains eligible and may lead; journal record quantity is never a score bonus.
- **Rejected impractical recommendations:** repeated voluntary 'too much preparation' feedback favors eligible faster alternatives, not overrides of allergies; dismissal ≠ diagnosed intolerance.
- **Corrected/revoked lab:** explanation/action source revisions retract; recompute priorities and related-marker context.
- **Goals only/no reliable personal source:** deliver a specific safe *general* action when one exists, not fabricated personal context.
- **Overlapping candidate actions:** merge similar interventions, show one coherent suggestion with true supporting goals.
- **Medical guard:** a clinically sensitive measurement invokes independently reviewed professional discussion rather than an unreviewed diet optimization rule.
- **Cross-domain decision reversal:** after relevant data correction or priority change, selected leading opportunity may change, with explanation/version history.
- **User-understanding test:** in real browser/DB flow, user can say what leads, why, source and uncertainty, rejected alternative/tradeoff and one action path; doesn't need to decipher math.

**Comparative test condition:** Validate against simple evidence-based generic baseline and deterministic single-domain baseline using **independently reviewed** scientific scenarios and feasible alternatives. Include safety failure counts, relevance, eligibility, data correctness, task success and recorded disagreement/reviewer rationale. No claim to 'highest impact' from arbitrary weighted scores or synthetic tests; genuinely uncoached external human participant findings must be separately recorded as PASS/FAIL/NOT TESTED. Real use metrics ≠ biological outcome effect. Do not treat model-suggested input as measured source.
