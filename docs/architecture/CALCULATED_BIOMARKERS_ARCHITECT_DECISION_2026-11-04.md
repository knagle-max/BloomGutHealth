# Bloom — Chief Architect decision: Calculated Biomarkers & Biological Ratios
**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Status:** APPROVED BOUNDED DEVELOPMENT SCOPE; NOT validated clinical interpretation, code completion, legal approval or launch sign-off.  
**Sources:** `Bloom_Biology_Calculated_Biomarkers_Specification_2026-11-04.md` (36 candidate formulas, one explicitly excluded), `Bloom_User_Testing_Calculated_Biomarkers_Ratios_Review_2026-11-04.md` (UT-CALC-4101–4112; CALC-T01–T16). **Canonical location:** My Biology → Bloodwork → Calculated Insights; add contextual links in grouped Bloodwork/Progress. DO NOT implement a second lab database, alternative correction history, unrelated score engine or independent dashboard.

## 1. Architect's binding decisions

**APPROVE** a versioned registry of deterministic derived observations tied to current immutable laboratory records; unit- and specimen-safe eligibility; explicit formula/reference versions; source/collection linkage, historical correction and withdrawal propagation; evidence/copy and permission gates. Every result must visibly say *Calculated from your laboratory results*, **not measured by the laboratory**. Separate (1) numerical computation eligibility from (2) eligibility for guideline category or personal interpretation. Mathematical ability to calculate never authorizes diagnosis or recommendations.

**REJECT** universal red/green ratios, an aggregate biological-health score, threshold invention, diagnosing sepsis/cancer/CKD/fibrosis/diabetes from derived metrics, fixed “optimal NLR”/“optimal HOMA,” genetic or microbiome attribution, inference from mismatched draws, and replacing source analytes with derivatives.

**DEFER** high-consequence guideline-stage/risk labels until the exact algorithm, applicability (age, population, illness, method, sex where required, fasting, jurisdiction, source reference) and actual UI copy receive Biology/qualified clinical and Legal intended-use review. Numeric calculation with neutral explanation may be viable before interpretation approval; some S/X metrics should remain hidden in ordinary mode. No additional paid API or licence (Oxford HOMA2) authorized.

## 3. Canonical contract and safety prerequisites (P0 per enabled feature)

- **Dependency graph:** immutable source observation IDs and revision IDs; analyte semantic identity; canonical numerical values and original units; specimen/panel/collection event, occurrence/date/time and fasting evidence; method and provenance; effective source state and correction history; linked guideline/interpretation versions.
- **No silent matching:** Never pair same-calendar-date different draws. Unknown collection time is not a licence to infer common specimen. A user correction can restore eligibility only when scientifically justified and explicitly confirmed, never by fabricating metadata.
- **Input validation:** positive/zero/negative policy per analyte, finite arithmetic, denominator > 0, suitable units and documented conversions; distinguish not measured, unknown, missing, measured zero and not applicable. No calculated value `Infinity`/`NaN`.
- **Lifecycle:** source edit/unit correction/date revision/deletion/retraction/import conflict immediately invalidates affected current projections; deterministic recompute from canonical active records, preserve auditable superseded versions; do not rewrite laboratory entries as calculated observations or leak one user's derived value to another.
- **Comparability:** formula version/variant, specimen method, fasting status, equipment/assay and clinical context may invalidate trend comparison. Split charts/hide “improved” assertions instead of false line continuity. Parent abnormal analytes are never visually superseded by normal-looking ratios.
- **Presentation:** default a small set of currently eligible and nonredundant insights (UT suggests 3–5), plus See all. Status: calculated, needs input, limited interpretation, research-only, specialist context. Plain English in UI, not raw G/R/D/X/S codes; source link and formula available via disclosure. Missing labs show informative prerequisites, never a test-buy nudge.
- **Guideline category gate:** FIB-4, ACR, eGFR and non-HDL context-sensitive categories require pinned guideline/cohort/age/method restrictions and copy approval before traffic bands. Absence of validation yields raw factual value where safe or unavailable, never a universal diagnosis.
- **Access and exports:** account-scoped derived result and source links; export marked CALCULATED with formula version, source references, units, date, caveat; no auto-sharing and no coach access without appropriate permission.
- **UI testing:** 320px and 200% zoom; light/dark; actual keyboard, NVDA/VoiceOver, chart text equivalent and return focus; low-numeracy teach-back on calculated-versus-measured, “normal ratio does not cancel abnormal parent” and when inference is unavailable.
- **User context:** TRT/medication use, muscle mass, age, acute illness and fasting evidence are relevant confounders for select measures; record if verified, never assume a personal health fact from app settings. Do not let ratios direct medical treatment.

## LIP-08 amendment — Friedewald historical LDL estimation (2026-11-04)

**Architect decision: APPROVE Friedewald as a method-specific optional calculated LDL-C variant**, not a substitute for laboratory-reported LDL-C or a new universal reference standard. The original Biology handoff named Sampson/NIH and Martin–Hopkins under LIP-08 but did not explicitly enumerate Friedewald; this amendment fills that omission. Keep one `LIP-08` domain contract with `formula_variant` values `friedewald`, `sampson_nih`, `martin_hopkins` rather than three incompatible sources of truth. No new parallel lab table.

**Equations:** `LDL-C(mg/dL) = TC - HDL-C - TG/5`; for standard mmol/L input the conventional expression is `LDL-C(mmol/L) = TC - HDL-C - TG/2.2`. Conversion factors are approximations tied to the conventions; implementation tests must pin exact conversions and rounding. Use source-identical TC, HDL-C and TG from one eligible lipid collection/panel and explicitly record original units, method, formula version and source IDs.

**Eligibility and limits:** Friedewald is conventionally not applicable when TG ≥400 mg/dL (approximately 4.5 mmol/L), and its accuracy can be poor at elevated TG and low LDL-C even below that level; respect relevant method-specific limits, fasting/lipemia/source quality and clinical context. Do not use an unsupported estimate in a case where a direct measured LDL-C is already supplied, without explicit comparison designation. Never calculate zero/negative LDL-C as an automatically valid biological conclusion. Do not infer a precise individual risk classification from the equation alone.

**UX and acceptance:** Existing reported LDL-C remains primary; if a calculated LDL-C is shown, label `Estimated LDL-C (Friedewald)` with inputs/formula, known limitations and source version. Historical comparisons retain recorded calculation method; never present a Friedewald-to-Sampson algorithm change as a true physiological change. For eligible panels, compare methods optionally with clear separate labels, not three conflicting 'actual LDL' cards. Preserve source corrections, unit changes, export provenance and source availability.

**Tests:** include TC=5.0, HDL=1.0, TG=1.1 mmol/L → Friedewald LDL-C=3.5 mmol/L under conventional divisor 2.2; matched mg/dL counterpart; TG beyond accepted limit → unavailable with reason; missing/mismatched input → unavailable; direct lab-reported LDL precedence; method-change trend split; source correction recompute.

**Priority:** P1 under LIP-08 after the first seven initial descriptive/guarded metrics; may be implemented earlier if specifically needed to preserve a historical laboratory method, but not ahead of active v0.5 correctness. Exact method applicability and medical copy remain subject to appropriate science/clinical review before risk interpretation.
