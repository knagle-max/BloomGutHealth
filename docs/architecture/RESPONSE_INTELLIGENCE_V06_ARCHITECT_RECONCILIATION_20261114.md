### Minimum viable genuinely useful response — each supported marker
1. **What changed:** source-linked before/after result, dates, units and assay/fasting caveat; contextual changes in relevant related markers, not isolated direction badges.
2. **What may have contributed:** structured review of *actual confirmed events* or explicit retrospective responses, biomarker-specific exposure windows and an approved population-level evidence map. Show 0–3 plausible contributors grouped by evidence/applicability and timing, explaining *why each is plausible* and whether it was recorded versus recalled. If evidence is insufficient, offer useful general evidence-based actions in a separately labelled `What generally helps` section, not fabricated `What caused yours`.
3. **What to continue / adjust / reconsider:** one or two **conditional, safe, concrete** recommendations that have action, rationale, source, scope and tradeoff. Example: if an increased walking routine is documented, and no contrary safety/clinical context is known, `Consider continuing your walking routine as one evidence-supported cardiometabolic habit; the lab results don't isolate its individual contribution`. Review relevant LDL/ApoB and symptoms before describing an overall outcome as better. Avoid `stop doing` a useful habit solely because a value changed; for an optional, potentially adverse diet/supplement choice recommend `review or discuss` until enough information exists. **Never advise starting/stopping/adjusting a prescription or treating a disorder based on observed association.**
4. **What is missing:** one highest-value targeted question, e.g. `Was this measurement fasting, and was the previous one?` or `Did your diet or medication change before this collection?`, if genuinely decision-relevant, with `Unknown / Not tracked / Prefer not to answer`. Save event-time precision separately from answered-at time. Updating the answer should recompute an explicitly revised, cited explanation.
5. **One-touch next action:** an actual existing routine, meal/planning, context-review, follow-up appointment discussion or source correction pathway; no inert `Learn more` hero. One primary action, optional alternate. Suggested action ≠ performed action.
