## Decision / stage authority
**Stage 1 — observational release eligible.** From Biology → Bloodwork → specific marker history, or Progress named lab comparison: show previous and latest **compatible** observations with original/canonical units, exact specimen/collection dates, lab identity, source revisions, method and fasting when known. Calculate an absolute difference and percentage only when original analyte identity, unit conversion and methodological comparability are proven. If missing/uncertain, say `Not directly comparable` and why; do not pretend a range limit or mathematical decrease equals medical improvement. Show an optional chronological list of verified *recorded* food, activity, body mass, check-in, trial and other events whose occurrence windows precede the draw. Include explicit empty context; user may add retrospective recall with date range uncertainty and its actual recorded-at timestamp.

**Stage 2 — reviewed explanatory hypotheses only.** If source-documented pre-draw changes and curated population-level evidence exist, show up to 2–3 **plausible contributors grouped**, without apportioning effect or ranking likelihood; link evidence and limitations, alternatives (fasting, method, recent illness, medications, natural variation etc.) and conflicting outcomes (e.g. falling TG while LDL/apoB rises). One optional decision-relevant question at a time with `Unknown`, `Not tracked`, `Prefer not to answer` and dismiss. One feasible next action: `Review changes`, `Monitor`, `Record context`, `Discuss with clinician`, or maintain a previously confirmed safe routine only after vetted tradeoff checks. Do not generate laboratory context or clinical instructions from a generic language model; model outage should leave Stage 1 intact. Claims must be independently reviewed by marker/assay/population/jurisdiction.

**Stage 3 — research gated.** N-of-1, statistical effect/individual-response calibration, genetic/microbiome mechanistic inferences, personalized causal conclusions and automatic efficacy rankings require a prespecified appropriate protocol, adequate repeated measurements, validated methods, confounder/carryover assessment, safety/oversight and prospective evaluation. These are NOT implied by successful Stage 1/2 code or quarterly labs. No invented RCV values; use only validated analyte/assay-specific parameterization (never one universal RCV), and distinguish expected variation from clinical meaningfulness.

## Canonical data contract / version & privacy
- **No new laboratory record ledger.** Reuse active lab observations, original-source metadata, revisions/receipts, `labHistory`, trend data, existing own-scoped Food/activity/trial/check-in APIs and evidence registry.
- Add only minimal owner-scoped **versioned annotation of exposure occurrence**, if existing canonical event storage cannot express it: event type/source, occurrence timestamp or bounded uncertain range, `recordedAt` timestamp, prospective vs `Added from memory`, actual vs planned vs proposed, appropriate units/dose/effort, revision and source identity. Absence is unknown, never zero or proof of continued adherence. Do not infer an event from a scheduled routine.
- Derived comparison/explanation may be computed on demand or cache by lab IDs+revisions, eligible event IDs+revisions, evidence version and authenticated owner. Correction, report deletion, event deletion, permission revocation, change of source, different account and stale async response invalidate active derived output. Historical provenance may survive only under permitted retention policy; withdrawn sensitive data must never remain in active display or unauthorized exports.
- Consent-scoped cross-product reads; no automatic third-party health disclosure and no clinical model inference on genetics, Viome pathway scores or personal SCFA/GLP1 flux.
- Recorded event time is distinct from entry time. **No post-specimen future leakage**. Window depends on marker, source and exposure; no default generic rolling 90-day causal hypothesis. Several co-occurring changes cannot be assigned distinct causal shares.
