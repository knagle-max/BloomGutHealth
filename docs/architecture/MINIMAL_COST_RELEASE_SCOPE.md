# Bloom minimal-cost release scope

2026-10-29 · LEG-HOBBY-1101 / LEG-WELL-1001,1005 · proposal for review, not launch or legal clearance.

**Purpose:** help adults optimize ordinary diet, training and recovery using goals, preferences and reliable, relevant observations. No diagnosis, disease detection/prevention/monitoring/treatment, medication or therapeutic dosing, or promised personal biological effects. Canada is the proposed initial market; age/market eligibility and actual public claims require owner and qualified review. No US or other-market expansion is assumed. Existing deployment is an authenticated development/review service; authentication alone does not establish invitation-only enrollment or public-launch approval.

**Budget:** preserve the current app, database, reviewed source adapters and feature registry. No paid provider, standing clinical staff, medical-device/QMS programme, research institution, expensive key migration or new recurring contract is assumed. Any material cost, new territory or consequential public capability returns to the owner with value, costs and low-cost alternatives. Future clinical/research infrastructure is deferred; current privacy, truthful claims, source rights and material safety remain essential.
