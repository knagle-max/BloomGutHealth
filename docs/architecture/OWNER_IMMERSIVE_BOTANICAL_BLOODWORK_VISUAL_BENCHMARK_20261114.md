## Binding design grammar / what to reproduce
1. **Immersive hero:** deep evergreen layered background with subtle organic macro texture and natural falloff; biologically plausible first-party generated anatomy/plant photography. Place one illustrative focal subject off-center with clear negative space for type; gradient/text contrast validated at actual viewport. Avoid neon sci-fi, cartoon 3D, dramatic excessive blur, visual noise or text embedded in generated image.
2. **Editorial signature:** rich high-contrast but comfortable ivory/cream Newsreader-like serif for major headings/system titles; confident but uncluttered sans-serif supporting copy and restrained letter-spaced small labels. Support long localized headings, text scaling and screen readers. Keep important metadata legible—not tiny overlines.
3. **Low-chrome system rows:** generous rounded corners, semi-transparent forest glass/paper layering, subtle inset line/elevation, left illustrated thumbnail with softly feathered edges, a concise title and at most one essential secondary line, clear chevron affordance and 44px+ interaction target. Avoid actual `backdrop-filter` performance overuse; use an equivalent simple styled surface where needed. Maintain true scroll and focus.
4. **Scientific imagery is designed as part of layout:** generated red cell/liver/bone/heart figures with distinct source-correct organism/anatomy rendering, consistent lighting, soft atmospheric compositing, no false diagnostic certainty. Images are visual metaphors or educational illustrations and **do not substitute for personal assays**. Follow `AI_FIRST_VISUAL_ASSET_PROMPT_AND_PRODUCTION_STANDARD_20261114.md`; owner need not source photography.
5. **The content disappears into the experience, not into a collapsed access trap:** all real source rows, reporting dates, lab corrections, source details and critical controls remain reachable and readable in drilled-in pages. Keep existing summary-to-full-report contract and true user data/status.
6. **Polished supporting shell:** authentic existing Bloom logo, stable mode picker/profile/menu/add/mode settings/nav, tactile icons, safe-area mobile footer and predictable desktop sidebar. The screenshot's three-icon bottom bar is **not authorization to drop Food, Explore, Profile, Activity, Coach or Athlete flows**. Preserve App.tsx/AppNavigation/BottomNav functionality, and make all important destinations navigable without URL typing.
7. **Whole product, distinct modes:** immersive botanical design DNA and similar tactile atmosphere can be used for major headers and high-value science sections, while long Food/form/table workflows use calm lighter or dark solid readable surfaces. **Everyday green**, **Athlete existing blue**, **Coach existing purple**—same material/typography/photographic style, different approved accent identities. No coercive global dark theme: preserve the user's light/dark preference, with **equally intentional, richly layered light mode** rather than reverting to boring white cards.

## Precise tradeoffs / design protections
- Hero/landing pages may be atmospheric, but **do not put long inputs or dense bloodwork tables on leafy photographs**. Use quiet flat paper/forest surfaces for precise reading and keyboard forms; drama on entry, calm in detail.
- Keep body text, values, units, dates and source caveats high-contrast. Avoid translucent panels that fail contrast or costly blur scrolling. At 320px, long categories wrap, meta lines may be shortened but full counts/history remain drillable. Dark mode needs actual contrast, not merely reduced brightness.
- Avoid image clutter: one art-directed dominant subject per hero, restrained system thumbnails with varied accurate subject, whitespace between groups, no uniform thumbnail icon overload.
- Not all routes need the screenshot's exact layout. Today and Biology/Explore benefit most from immersive hero; Food from real food photography on tactile surfaces; Progress from a serious legible clinical ledger with editorial hero and atmospheric accents.
- Preserve existing functional requirements, actual metrics/claim guards and purpose/privacy permissions. Example words/numbers in screenshots are **not** binding data requirements. No redesign effort should be consumed debating example scores; developer simply binds actual data or safe placeholders.
- Accessibility: do not implement prototype's `user-scalable=no`; support 200% scaling, keyboard, screen readers, motion reduction, mobile safe areas, 320/390/768/1440 and desktop; test loaded/unavailable artwork and slow networks.
