# Bloom product direction

Everyday is the planned free core. Athlete is the planned subscription addition at go-to-market. Both are currently accessible without billing or paywalls. A mode choice is a workspace preference, never proof of a paid entitlement. A future subscription launch needs separate server-enforced entitlements, billing/provider webhooks, grace/expiry handling and tests; the biology registry, personal health records and data export must not be duplicated across plans. Do not infer paid status from athlete level or the client toggle.

The core experience is Today → Explore → Progress. Logging, planning and data entry are tools reached from these screens, not separate biological models. Athlete keeps its distinct blue identity and performance workflows while using the same reference knowledge and personal measurements.

The central reference registry lives in PostgreSQL: immutable version identifier/fingerprint, entities, relationships, sources and relationship-source joins. Personal genes, laboratory reports, microbiome reports, journal entries and experiments remain separately scoped/encrypted as already designed. A registry claim is never itself a measurement in a person. The initial registry carries forward reviewed source relationships and a bounded set of food families, not arbitrary clinical knowledge. Existing recommendation rules remain authoritative; the food explanation shows which relationships actually participate and which remain educational. Migrating every legacy rule into a declarative evaluator is future work.

New knowledge requires a versioned code/data change and evidence review. Each connection carries evidence class, applicability, direction/context, limitations, contradiction-review status and citations. “Not systematically reviewed” is explicit; lack of a contradictory citation is not presented as unanimity. AI may explain structured results but must not invent evidence, personalized effects, quantities or new recommendation rules.

Development sequence remains: reliability → biological relationships/food explanations → deeper personalization → coaching/substitutions → outcome validation → integrations/research expansion. Existing imports, missing-value handling, recalculation and encrypted persistence stay covered by regression checks. Broad supplement interaction screening, automatic lab-report OCR, stool/Bristol tracking, comprehensive microbiome harmonization, wearable integration, live grocery stock/prices, research surveillance and digital-twin simulation are not claimed complete by the initial relationship release.

NIH's Nutrition for Precision Health is a research direction, not validation or endorsement of Bloom: https://commonfund.nih.gov/nutritionforprecisionhealth. The international microbiome-testing consensus supports being explicit about limited clinical utility: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39647502/.

Free-core monetization remains undecided. Any future affiliate relationship should be clearly disclosed and separated from evidence grading and recommendation priority. No ads, affiliate links, health-data sales or paid recommendation ranking are introduced here.
