## 2. Brand palette and material grammar

Everyday use forest green and ivory: functional original `#246B50`, deep ink `#173E32`, deepest green `#102B25`, warm paper `#F5F3EB`, sage `#D9E7D5`, warm white `#FFFEFA`, muted clay `#D4A37C`; these are existing **suggested design anchors**, not arbitrary new runtime CSS overrides. Image left field generally deep green shadow, often nearly `#102B25`; right features warm amber/golden natural window/sunlight and tangible ivory, wood, glass, botanical greens, dew and a little muted berry/lilac for richness. Keep images natural rather than forcing every pixel to a UI token.

Athlete: original blue `#0F1B2E` base, `#182B45` navy surfaces, `#81BEFF` accent, white-blue text `#F1F6FF`; natural greens/rocks can remain physically plausible, sportswear and atmosphere support blue identity. Coach, when explicitly enabled/authorized, uses existing purple family, **not** recolored green/blue. Test actual light/dark contrast instead of assuming a photo's dark region guarantees readability.

**Optics/materials:** For landscapes and environmental scenes start with a plausible **35mm f/2.8** photographic language, environmental low-angle sunrise / directional side lighting, modest shallow depth, subtle grain. For still-life or food, **50–60mm f/4** guidance, accurate hydration, ceramic imperfections and textile fibers; for botanical details use appropriate closer focus. Real surface contact and physically plausible shadows. Optics are stylistic prompt cues, NEVER invented camera metadata proving a real photograph. Avoid generic '8k', glossy 3D, plastic skin, wax, neon, generic wellness stock, fake bokeh silhouettes, overdone glows/particles, floating unexplained objects, oversaturation, random technical UI.

**Composition:** Hero is an intentionally wide original usually 16:9, except exact asset-specific requirements (BVA-0051 **2:1**, 1600×800). Subject and interest are in the **right 55–65%**. Left **35–45%** has naturally dark low-detail negative space for live heading, short purpose/deck and genuine actions. Crops must not cut the signature subject. Avoid overly dense left-side foliage or subject on top of overlay zone. Keep page hero inside existing canonical shared wrapper: softly rounded image, kicker, large editorial serif title, supporting live context and meaningful DOM buttons; preserve actual global navigation. The image itself has **no text, logos, charts, counts, labels, input controls, score dials or watermarks**.

On 320/390px mobile, a portrait subject crop alone often **loses the left negative space**. Thus either create **separate art-directed mobile render** with upper/lower text-safe field or use a **separate accessible live text panel/gradient**. Do NOT call a center-cropped portrait source overlay-ready by default. Never scale mobile blindly from the desktop; test 320/390/768/1024/1440 actual DOM UI and both light/dark themes.

## 3. Repeatable end-to-end procedure for every BVA hero

1. **Read actual source**: Fetch the current entry `BVA-NNNN` from `docs/visual-assets/visual-asset-registry.json` **at target branch HEAD**. Capture name, purpose, route, dimensions/aspect, modes, detailed `productionBriefV2` or `descriptiveBrief`, exclusions, existing file paths, integration/review flags and owner directives. Check `client/src/content/editorial-media.ts` and any preceding/related BVA images; decide whether existing work is a reuse candidate, approved adjacent scene or actual unmet new requirement. Avoid manufacturing a second registry.
2. **Preserve old design**: Old files and accepted science art are not overwritten before approval/integration. Keep registry ID stable for replacement. Cross-check relevant screenshots/current live route for format/meaning; if not accessible, record visual baseline as unverified.
3. **Art-direct the story**: Choose ONE immediately recognizable physical focal narrative *specific to this route*, and 1–3 supporting objects max if possible. Respect specific owner exclusions and scientific meaning. Visual unity comes from natural photography, left-field negative space, golden daylight, tactile materials; distinction comes from subject and setting. Example Today landscape, Food food photo, Biology natural-history anatomy, Explore discovery table, Athlete runner.
4. **Prompt precisely**: Describe focal subject, count/object geometry and placement, physical setting, material properties and imperfections, optics/aperture/light direction, palette/mode, 16:9 or exact-specific aspect, negative-space coordinates, mobile crop concern, no embedded UI, no invented personal science. Negatives: plastic/airbrushed/wax-like, cheap CGI, overgloss, neon, floating unlabeled gadgets, anatomy errors, text, symbols, labels, watermark. For science imagery: clearly state conceptual metaphor versus literal anatomy; no precise strain/species or patient's biology without validated basis.
5. **Generate and inspect**: Use first-party image-generation capabilities when user requests a new art image; assess the ACTUAL returned frame, not a similar internet image or an image-query substitute. Reject subject mismatches outright (in prior attempts Food and Explore were accidentally regenerated instead of Biology/Athlete). If initial concept is wrong, iterate; owner approval of direction is NOT identical to final-image approval. Never fabricate a success from a failed generation.
6. **Present actual art for visual review**: Let owner compare the genuine created image; describe what works and identify weaknesses without claiming false scientific accuracy. Owner may approve a specific candidate, request iteration or approve direction only. Record exact art candidate chosen.
7. **Export source and responsive derivatives** after final approval: preserve original RGB PNG at actual intrinsic resolution (past five mostly ~1672×941; never misstate dims). Export high-quality optimized WebP desktop at original width plus ~1200 and 800, and mobile portrait variants ~768×1024 and ~576×1024 with deliberate anchored crop; optionally AVIF when proven by current tooling and host. Do not stretch images or claim portrait crop will safely host overlaid text by default. Include `README.md` with description, intended slot, safe-zone constraints and science/copyright cautions and a `manifest.json` SHA-256 + size + dimensions. ZIP with CRC check. Record content hash of final source and all variants.
8. **Publish persistent delivery**: Shared Library `/Bloom gut health/Agent Handoffs/`, confirm EXACT returned upload filename (Library may suffix `(1)` on duplicates). Update EXISTING `docs/visual-assets/visual-asset-registry.json` entry with **owner-approved ARTWORK stage only**, actual package path, dates, hashes/manifest and `NOT_INTEGRATED`; preserve old `sourceRelation` and old runtime mapping. Create per-ID GitHub handoff at `docs/visual-assets/BVA_NNNN_..._APPROVED_REPLACEMENT_DELIVERY_YYYYMMDD.md` with exact files, approval decision, meaning, crop caveats, QA instructions; link this from agents when useful.
9. **Codex implementation and genuine acceptance**: Codex acquires ZIP, verifies hashes, imports into approved first-party path, updates canonical media slot and registry without duplicating IDs; retains old bytes as rollback; validates 320/390/768/1024/1440 with actual UI, all applicable themes, focus, touch, screen readers, zoom, optimized loading/fallback; checks source/clinical/copyright/rights; records screenshot evidence at exact app SHA. Only after real app tests mark INTEGRATED/VERIFIED. An archive and design approval are NOT deployment.

**Recommended minimal prompt template** (adapt subject and exact slot, don't blindly copy):
> Original Bloom [ID/surface] editorial hero art: [one precise recognisable physical scene with accurate subjects/actions] in [credible setting, physical materials and imperfections]. Main subject located right of frame, [specific details/secondary objects], left 40–45% softly dark [forest or blue] atmospheric quiet zone without high-contrast shapes, safe for overlay text. Directional natural [sun/window] illumination from [direction] at [time], [appropriate lens/f-stop] photographic language, subtle grain, plausible depth and physical contact, premium warm botanical natural-history mood. [Exact palette, output aspect, size and responsive focal point]. Image artwork only; absolutely no text, logo, captions, UI, measurements, data, watermarks, misleading microscopy, incorrect anatomy, generic gloss or artificial CGI. Describe abstract biology as illustrative, not sampled reality.

## 4. Scientific, safety, ownership and interface gates

- Decorative hero can communicate a scientific **theme**, not a personal result. No microbe labels/species identity, abundance, causality, pathogen severity, personalized health state, treatment improvement, fake assay, success/green status. If recognizable cellular structures, organs or living organisms are depicted, require Biology to examine scientific plausibility. Original source art may be creative, but labels in explanatory UI must not overclaim.
- Avoid intimate or identifiable real individuals where art provenance lacks explicit rights; AI generative human figures are editorial only. Commercial use and provider terms require review/record; never promise exclusivity.
- Keep actual page body/feature information and flows untouched by art replacement; the designer supplies images and visual skin, not clinical logic or IA.
- Do not embed health records, user data or inferred metrics into third-party generators. Use synthetic/non-personal visuals.
- Visual review separately considers **brand**, **page-specific usefulness**, **scientific/rights accuracy**, **runtime accessibility** and **actual image rendering**; do not make generic 'Approved' imply all five.
