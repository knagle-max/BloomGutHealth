# Content and science communication standards — Proposed

**Core line:** “Precision nutrition made practical.” Tagline candidate: “Understand your biology. Nourish your potential.” Marketing positioning is not a clinical efficacy claim.

### Copy hierarchy
1. An understandable action or descriptive fact.
2. Why relevant to the user's stated goal **only if actual rules support this**.
3. Data provenance and completeness.
4. Evidence classification and relevance to people.
5. Scientific limitations/competing findings and source access.

### Language rules
- Prefer “may,” “has been associated with,” “contains,” “was measured/reported,” “in this study,” depending on evidence; don't blanket-qualify verified nutrient composition.
- Never say “your bacteria are producing more butyrate,” “you have a healthy gut score,” “this genotype means you're deficient,” “anti-doping cleared,” or “your recovery is optimized” without validated personal evidence.
- Make uncertain and unavailable visible. Unknown, not collected, no evidence, conflicting evidence and absent result are distinct states.
- Nutrition targets, minimum requirements and limits have distinct meanings. Partially reported totals cannot demonstrate that a nutrient limit is met.
- Include dates and units for labs, and avoid drawing comparisons across incompatible assay methods without scientific approval.
- No disease diagnosis, individualized supplement prescription or clinical treatment assurance.
- Do not imply NIH / clinical organization endorsement of Bloom or validated microbiome-based meal personalization.
- Avoid shame, fear, “bad food”, punitive streaks, and false urgency. Use helpful, autonomy-supporting prompts.

### Example
**Preferred:** “Oats contain beta-glucan, a fermentable fibre. Some human studies have examined its digestive and metabolic effects. Bloom can show the relevant evidence and whether any reviewed rule contributes to your current food priority.”

**Avoid:** “Oats improve your unique microbiome and boost your butyrate production.”

### External-facing claims release gate
Require Biology Agent approval for any nutrition benefit, performance effect, microbiome effect, disease relation or cited evidence level. Require Security Agent approval for any privacy, encryption, clinical-data sharing or AI-photo processing statement. Developer to confirm final marketing words match shipped behaviors; distinguish demo/synthetic examples.
