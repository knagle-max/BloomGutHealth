Understanding result statuses

Measured & matched

A source genotype is verified and matches the accepted reference semantics. This alone does not imply a health effect.

Not measured

This assay contains no usable call for the variant; absence is not a reference genotype.

Could not resolve orientation

A strand/build ambiguity prevents reliable matching; Bloom must not silently flip the call.

Unsupported variation type

An ordinary SNP file cannot determine an untyped repeat, structural deletion or haplotype.

Conflicting source calls

Different uploads disagree; both originals stay available and the interpretation is withheld.

Research only

Educational research is available, but a personalized action is not approved.
