# Calculated Insights — UX acceptance plan (not executed)

## Critical
1. Enter ANC 3.4 and ALC 1.6 from same CBC: display 2.13, `Calculated`, inspect original sources and no immune optimization verdict.
2. ANC or ALC missing, ALC zero or repeated draws same day: no invalid ratio; no infinity; no cross-draw pairing.
3. Fasting glucose 5 mmol/L and insulin 10 µIU/mL: HOMA1 2.22 only if fasting/collection matched; cannot claim diagnosis or HOMA2.
4. FIB-4 age 45 fixture ≈1.42; age 33 no validated band; age 70 uses different policy; acute illness suppresses automatic interpretation.
5. TC 5.5 and HDL 1.0 mmol/L: non-HDL 4.5 mmol/L, parent values retain meaningful status and visibility.
6. TG/HDL conventional mmol/L ratio 2 for 1.8/0.9; mg/dL-derived ratio is different, threshold not silently shared.
7. Urine albumin 30 mg/L / urine creatinine 10 mmol/L → ACR 3 mg/mmol. Serum creatinine must not be selected. No one-result CKD diagnosis.
8. Iron 15 and TIBC 60 µmol/L: TSAT 25%.
9. Correct unit/date/source and verify all dependents, charts and exports updated from exact linked source. Preserve superseded derivations.
10. Parent analyte is abnormal but ratio unremarkable: user must not infer the entire panel is normal.
11. No labs, missing inputs, wrong source, inaccessible report and offline states are actionable without invented values or testing-purchase pressure.
12. 320px/200% zoom, VoiceOver/NVDA, keyboard navigation, dark mode, slow network and export show parity and preserve history.
13. Exploratory PLR, MLR, SII remain under Advanced without clinical grades or duplicate notifications.
14. Verify calculated result copying/export label, source IDs, version, date and caveat, never presented as measured blood test.

## Participants and signoff
>=5 uncoached formative users with low/high lab literacy plus assistive technology participants. Record observed tasks, time, quote-level teach-back, build SHA, device and screen reader. A hazardous inference blocks the corresponding clinical copy pending redesign and retest. AI walkthrough or static HTML preview does not satisfy real-world tests.
